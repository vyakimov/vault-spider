# Old Domain Cleanup

Letting a couple of unused personal domains lapse instead of renewing them.

## The Transition
I held three personal domains years ago when it was trendy to have your own site. One was for blogging (I posted twice), another for email (never actually used), and a third was just nostalgia. Each renewal was $12/year and I would get an email reminder and pay without thinking. A few months ago I realized I wasn't using any of them and let two lapse rather than auto-renewing.

## What Stuck
The main domain I use for personal projects is hosted, so it stays. The domain that actually receives mail (personal brand stuff) is also active. Letting go of the vanity domains was oddly freeing—it's less mental overhead. I still own my essential online identity, but I stopped maintaining the cruft. The email forwarding from the old domains is now off, so anyone using those addresses will just get bounces, but it's been years since that mattered.
