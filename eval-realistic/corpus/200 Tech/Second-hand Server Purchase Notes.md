# Second-hand Server Purchase Notes

What to check before buying used rack gear, learned from one bad purchase.

## The Mistake
I bought a cheap rack-mounted server from a surplus seller without inspecting it first. It arrived and the power supplies were suspect (burned smell), and one drive bay had a bad controller. Returning it was a hassle. Now I have a checklist before buying anything secondhand.

## The Checklist
First: ask for photos of the internals and serial numbers (to verify age). Second: confirm all original parts are included (power supplies, drive trays, RAM). Third: ask about the power draw and noise level (some enterprise servers are insanely loud). Fourth: check BIOS date and firmware version to get a sense of maintenance history. Finally: avoid anything older than 7 years if it's going to be always-on (power efficiency degrades). I learned this the hard way, and it's saved me money since.
