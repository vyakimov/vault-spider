# Newsletter Unsubscribe Sweep

A periodic pass unsubscribing from newsletters that never get read.

## The Habit
Every quarter I scan my email's "Newsletters" folder (a rule routes all mail flagged as newsletter to this folder automatically). I check which ones I've actually opened and read in the past three months. Anything I've scrolled past 5+ times without opening gets unsubscribed immediately. This keeps the noise manageable without me having to make real-time decisions about email.

## Results
I went from subscribing to 20 newsletters down to 7 that I actually read. The unread count stays below 10 now. Some newsletters I genuinely miss and re-subscribe to when I'm in the mood (usually during a slow period). The mental model is: newsletters are for when I have time to absorb, not for FOMO-driven urgency. If I miss the news because I unsubscribed, that's fine—the truly important stuff will find me through RSS or conversation.
