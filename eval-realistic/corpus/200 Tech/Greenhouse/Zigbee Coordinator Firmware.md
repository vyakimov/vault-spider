The CC2531 is running firmware version 2.7.8, released mid-2024. Updates are available (currently at 2.8.1), but I've postponed upgrading because the pairing process is finicky — have to erase flash, reprogram via USB, and rejoin every device. Given that the mesh is stable and I rarely add new sensors, the cost-benefit isn't there. If I hit a critical bug (security patch, major stability issue), I'll bite the bullet and re-pair everything. The coordinator has been stable for 8+ months, so no urgency.
