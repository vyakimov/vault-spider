Matter is the new standard pushing out Zigbee, Z-Wave, and proprietary protocols. It's more open and less fragmented, which is good. But Matter devices are still rare in budget tiers (where I shop), and the ecosystem is immature. Zigbee2MQTT is battle-tested and I have existing sensors. For new devices, I'm watching Matter adoption — if the price parity holds and device selection improves, I'd migrate, but I'm not repairing existing Zigbee infrastructure just to say "I use Matter." The Thread backbone (IP-based mesh) is more robust than Zigbee's 802.15.4, but I don't have a Thread border router yet. Decision: stick with Zigbee for now, revisit in 2027 when Matter supply-chain stabilizes.
