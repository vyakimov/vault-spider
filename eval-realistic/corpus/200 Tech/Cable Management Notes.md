# Cable Management Notes

Reorganizing the desk cabling after one too many accidental unplugs.

## The Problem
Cables ran in a tangled mess behind the desk. Every time I moved the chair or adjusted the monitor, I'd catch a cable and disconnect something. Power went out, ethernet dropped, keyboard stopped working. It was chaos. I couldn't remember what each cable was for and kept following the same tangles trying to find the right one.

## The Solution
I grouped cables by destination: monitor cable + USB runs to the external monitor in one bundle, power cables in a separate bundle, ethernet and USB hub cables in another. Velcro straps every few inches, and a label maker to tag each cable at both ends. Each bundle has its own path to the outlets. Now I can unplug one thing without touching anything else. The desk is less cluttered and troubleshooting is faster. The label maker paid for itself in sanity the first week.
