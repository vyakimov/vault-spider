# Podcast Queue Management

How the podcast queue is kept from becoming an anxiety-inducing backlog that feels like an obligation rather than entertainment.

## The Problem
After subscribing to a dozen podcasts, the queue grew into a 200+ episode backlog. The number felt overwhelming—I'd avoid listening because the queue intimidated me. This defeated the purpose of podcasts as a low-pressure way to stay informed and entertained.

## The Solution
I now archive episodes after 2 weeks and only keep the current week's releases in the queue. If I didn't listen to an episode in that window, I let it go. This reframes podcasts from a completionist obligation into a currently-relevant feed of interesting content. I listen to about 8-10 hours a week during commutes and chores, which means I sample most new episodes without getting behind. The queue never exceeds about 20 episodes now. The psychological shift—from backlog anxiety to "what's fresh this week"—has made podcasts enjoyable again.
