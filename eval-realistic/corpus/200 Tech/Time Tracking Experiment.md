# Time Tracking Experiment

A short-lived experiment tracking hours spent on different homelab projects to understand where effort goes.

## Setup and Initial Results
I used a simple time-tracking app for two months, logging each homelab session. Early results showed I spent far more time on maintenance and troubleshooting than on new projects—roughly 60% reactive work, 40% proactive. The biggest time sink was backup management and system updates, which I had underestimated. This data was genuinely useful for prioritization.

## Why It Stopped
After a couple of months the overhead of logging every session killed the momentum. The friction of stopping to mark time, then resuming work, became annoying. The time tracking itself started consuming time. I realized that the goal of understanding effort distribution was achieved and continuing the tracking wasn't adding value. Sometimes a short experiment is enough. The key insight—that maintenance dominates over new features—shaped how I now allocate weekend time.
