# Camera Doorbell Evaluation

A comparison of doorbell camera options, focusing on a local-only model versus cloud-dependent alternatives.

## Cloud-Dependent Options
The mainstream brands (Ring, Logitech) offer slick mobile apps and cloud storage of video clips. But they require recurring subscriptions, they store footage on servers I don't control, and if the company changes terms or gets acquired, I lose access. The outdoor camera becomes a liability if it depends on a third-party service being online.

## Local-Only Evaluation
I tested a local option that connects to the tailnet and streams video to a home server. No cloud subscription, no company dependency. The trade-off: slower setup, rougher interface, and I need to run my own NVR on LordByron to store footage. But the video stays under my control and the system works even if the internet is down. After testing, the local model won—the extra complexity is worth the autonomy. I installed it and integrated it with the home automation dashboard.
