# E-ink Tablet Notes

Using an e-ink tablet for reading long PDFs pulled from papertrail.

## The Use Case
I started scanning documents into papertrail (OCR receipts, manuals, technical specifications). Reading them on the laptop screen gets tiring after 20 minutes. The e-ink tablet has zero backlight and matches paper reading closely. I export PDFs from papertrail, push them to the tablet via USB, and read without strain.

## Workflow
It's intentionally simple: no stylus use, no handwriting recognition, just a basic PDF reader app and annotation. The battery lasts about two weeks between charges. Since it's not internet-connected, it's a quiet reading experience with no notifications or distractions. I've considered a tablet with a stylus for actual note-taking, but the plain reader is perfect for what I'm using it for. The device cost about $200 and has absolutely earned its place on the desk.
