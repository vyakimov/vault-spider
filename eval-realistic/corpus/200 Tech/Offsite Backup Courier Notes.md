# Offsite Backup Courier Notes

An informal but deliberate routine for rotating an offline backup disk between my home and a family member's house to maintain a geographically distant copy of critical data.

## The System
Every three months I format a 4TB USB drive with a fresh backup from the encrypted pool. I copy it to my cousin's house about an hour's drive away—she has a fireproof safe in a bedroom closet. I bring home the previous backup disk and verify it still reads correctly, then shelve it as a local archive. This way there's always a current backup off-site and a spare local copy at home.

## Handoff Protocol
I keep a spreadsheet tracking which disk is where and its last verification date. When I swap, I document the backup size, creation date, and encryption key fingerprint (stored separately in a password manager). This redundancy means I can survive a house fire or theft of all home equipment—the backup disk at my cousin's place has everything from three months back, which is acceptable RPO for my data.
