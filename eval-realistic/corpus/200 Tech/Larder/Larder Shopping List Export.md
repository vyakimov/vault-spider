The app can generate a shareable shopping list from items below a stock threshold. You set the threshold (e.g., "fewer than 2 jars of cumin") and it exports as a plain-text list with item names and quantities. The export includes a timestamp so you know when the list was generated. I usually send this to a shared notes app on my phone before heading to the store, and it syncs over the tailnet without ever leaving the LAN.
