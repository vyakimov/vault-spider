Backlog of half-baked ideas that probably won't ship:

- **Smart shopping list**: cross-reference expiry dates with stock levels to predict what's needed before the next trip. Never built because it's a marginal UX improvement and the heuristics are fragile (what if someone goes out of town?).
- **Bulk barcode import**: accept a CSV of barcodes and add them all at once. Sounds good but rarely needed — most imports come from individual scans.
- **Meal planning integration**: suggest recipes based on what's in stock. Abandoned after testing; the recipe database is too specific to my diet and the integration is just feature creep.
- **Freezer audit mode**: check items at regular intervals (weekly, monthly) with a checklist UI. Useful in theory but I've never actually used it when I built a prototype.

The app is pretty close to "done" in its current form. New features mostly just add complexity without solving real pain points.
