The barcode scanner works great on most items but fails predictably in a few cases:

- **Metallic barcodes**: sometimes printed on foil pouches or cans. The camera focuses on the shine, not the bars themselves.
- **Curved surfaces**: bottles and cans need to be held flat to the camera. A slight angle and the perspective distortion breaks the decode.
- **Worn/damaged barcodes**: older items or those stored near the freezer door where condensation builds up. Once the bars start to fade, the scanner can't get a read within 2-3 tries.
- **Low light**: the kitchen counter can be dim at certain times of day. Phone's auto-flash helps but sometimes I just retry in better light.
- **High-gloss label shine**: some product labels are too shiny; the camera's auto-exposure gets confused and underexposes the barcode itself.

About 5-10% of scans fail on the first try. I usually just flip the item or move to better light and retry. Not a deal-breaker.
