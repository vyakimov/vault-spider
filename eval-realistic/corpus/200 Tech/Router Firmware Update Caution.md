# Router Firmware Update Caution

A decision to delay router firmware updates by about a week after release, stemming from one particularly bad update that broke the network.

## The Bad Update Incident
Last year a firmware push bricked the router's WiFi radio on one band, leaving guests unable to connect and causing hours of troubleshooting. By the time I rolled back, the damage was done and I'd spent the evening on recovery. The firmware was pulled the next day after multiple reports, but the lesson stuck with me.

## Current Approach
Now I let firmware updates bake for a week after release, watching community forums for reports of bugs or breaking changes. If there's a critical security patch, I make an exception. Otherwise, waiting that week saves me from being an early adopter of a broken release. I test the update in the evening rather than the morning before work so I can troubleshoot if needed. The small delay in applying patches is worth the peace of mind that the update is stable.
