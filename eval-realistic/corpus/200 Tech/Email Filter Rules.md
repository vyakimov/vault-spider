# Email Filter Rules

Filter rules that keep the inbox usable without an aggressive auto-archive.

## The Categories
Anything with a "List-ID" header (mailing lists) goes to a folder. Notifications (GitHub, build system, deployments) get labeled and auto-marked read if they're not errors. Marketing mail gets auto-trashed. Personal emails from my core contacts stay in the inbox. Everything else lands in a staging area where I review it once a week.

## The Philosophy
I resisted auto-archive for years because I was worried about missing real emails. But having 200 unread messages is useless too—I ignore the count. The filter rules are specific: they match senders and subjects I've already decided are non-urgent. It took a few months to tune (I had to whitelist a legitimate service that looked like spam), but now the inbox stays at 10–20 real items most days. The staging folder handles the ambiguous stuff; I batch-process it weekly.
