# Notebook App Comparison

A summary of why Obsidian won out after I tested a couple of other notebook applications.

## Competitors Tested
I tried a popular commercial app that was smooth and polished but stored everything in their cloud by default. Migrating out would be a pain. A lightweight markdown editor was simple but lacked linking and the backlog of notes quickly became unnavigable. Both had trade-offs that bothered me—one too locked-in, one too minimal.

## Why Obsidian Won
Obsidian stores files locally in plain markdown, so I own the data completely. The backlink system and graph view make connecting notes natural. The vault stays in git for version control and backup. It's extensible without being overwhelming. The privacy model is clear and I'm not a product being monetized. After switching, I migrated hundreds of old notes from other apps and kept adding to the vault—a sign that the tool wasn't fighting me. Obsidian has been my primary notebook for over a year now and I'd be hesitant to change.
