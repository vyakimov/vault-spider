# Old Phone Repurposing

Turning a retired smartphone into a dedicated camera for capturing photos and uploading to the Cassowary photo storage system.

## Phone Selection and Setup
I took an old phone from a drawer, wiped it, and flashed a lightweight Android ROM to extend battery life. Paired it with a minimal data-only plan. Installed the Cassowary app and a file manager. Set it to charge on a USB plug near the kitchen so it's always ready. The camera sensor is decent enough for everyday documentation photos—better than my actual camera for quick snapshots.

## Workflow
When I want to record something (a shelf of organized tools, a baking project, a plant issue), I grab the dedicated phone instead of my primary device. Photos auto-upload to Cassowary when WiFi is available. This keeps my personal photo library separate from my work phone and means I'm not burning through battery on my main device. The old phone does one job well and takes up minimal space. At this point it's paid for itself in convenience.
