# Search Habits in This Vault

A reflection on how search is actually used day to day versus how I initially expected it to be used.

## Expected vs. Reality
I thought I'd use sophisticated saved searches and filters to build reports from the vault. In practice, that almost never happens. Instead, I use search to answer immediate questions: "Did I document how to reset the NAS?" or "Which notes mention the tailnet?" Quick full-text searches dominate. Tag-based navigation happens but is less frequent than I anticipated.

## Most Common Queries
Most searches are person-centric ("Marionette", "Larder") or problem-centric ("backup failed", "network timeout"). I rarely search by topic or date range. The most useful queries are the ones that pull up a note I partially remember but can't locate by folder. The vault's search catches matches in titles, content, and tags, which is sufficient for daily use. I've stopped trying to optimize the search experience and instead focus on clear note naming and consistent tagging—these small habits make search work well without overcomplicating it.
