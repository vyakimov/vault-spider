# Second Monitor Arm Notes

Desk setup notes from adding a monitor arm that frees up space and improves ergonomics.

## Installation
Adding an arm meant removing the monitor stand and clamping the arm to the back of the desk. This freed up about 8 inches of desk depth where the stand used to be. The secondary monitor now floats at eye level instead of sitting stacked awkwardly beside the primary. Cables route up and through the arm's cable management, keeping the desk surface cleaner.

## Ergonomic Changes
With both monitors at proper height and distance, neck strain during long coding sessions has decreased noticeably. The floating secondary screen can be adjusted throughout the day—pulled closer for video calls, angled further back when reading reference material. The arm's weight capacity is solid, supporting even the older, heavier 1080p monitor without sagging. The desk feels less cluttered and I've relocated the freed space to a keyboard tray that I keep in a drawer. Overall improvement for minimal cost.
