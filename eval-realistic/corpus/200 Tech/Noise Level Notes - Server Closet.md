# Noise Level Notes - Server Closet

Fan and drive noise measurements before and after a quieter case swap.

## The Problem
The server closet was loud—loud enough that with the door open, I could hear the fans from the living room. It was a mix of drive noise and fan noise. The old case was cheap metal that resonated. I decided to measure it properly and then do something about it.

## The Fix
I swapped to a case with sound dampening (thick foam lining inside). Added larger, slower fans to replace the noisy high-RPM ones. The result: the closet is now quiet enough that I only hear it if I press an ear to the door. Before the change, it was a constant hum. After: barely noticeable. The upgrade cost about $150 in fans and case modifications. Worth every penny for daily noise reduction. I measured with a phone dB app before and after: 78dB down to 62dB at a meter away.
