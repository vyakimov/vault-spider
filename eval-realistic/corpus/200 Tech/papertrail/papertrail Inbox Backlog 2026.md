Cleared about 400 unscanned pages in January and February by dedicating a weekend to running them through the document feeder. The backlog had accumulated over the holidays when I skipped mail processing for two weeks. Most were junk (credit-card offers, store circulars) but there were a few important statements mixed in. The OCR caught 95% of the text correctly, which was higher than I expected for aged paper with varying print quality. After the backlog was cleared, the system has stayed on top of new mail with weekly scans.
