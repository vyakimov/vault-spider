# Home Inventory for Insurance

A photo-and-list inventory of valuables kept on a private encrypted drive, separate from the homeowner's insurance policy, for claim purposes in case of theft or damage.

## Documentation Method
I photograph electronics, furniture, art, and jewelry with a high-resolution camera, noting serial numbers and purchase dates in a spreadsheet. Each item has a photo, estimated replacement cost, and any identifying marks (dent in the frame, monogram on the watch). I organized the spreadsheet by room and category for quick reference. Everything backs up to the encrypted pool on LordByron, kept offline on the backup drive, and in a printed copy in a fireproof box.

## Insurance Strategy
The inventory is submitted to the insurance company at policy renewal time. If there's ever a claim, I'll have documentation ready. For high-value items like electronics or heirlooms, I keep appraisal certificates or purchase receipts. The insurance agent suggested this kind of detailed inventory cuts claim-processing time by weeks and prevents disputes over valuation. It takes annual maintenance, but it's far easier to update now than to reconstruct after a loss.
