# Smart Lock Evaluation

A decision log on why I chose not to install a smart lock despite initially being interested, after reading about cloud dependencies.

## Initial Appeal
The convenience of unlocking the door from my phone seemed useful, especially during package deliveries or when my hands are full. Most smart locks have backup key access, so I wouldn't be stuck if the system failed. The setup looked straightforward and reviews were mostly positive.

## Why I Declined
After digging deeper, I found that most smart locks phone to home to a company server to verify the unlock request, even if the lock itself has local connectivity. A power outage or internet disruption and I cannot unlock my own door without the backup key. The liability felt unacceptable. I decided the mechanical deadbolt with a good old-fashioned key was more reliable, even if less trendy. I kept the mechanical lock and settled for unlocking the door the traditional way.
