# Mechanical Keyboard Notes

Switch and keycap choices for the keyboard used with PuddleJumper's desk setup.

## Current Build
I went with linear switches (Gateron Yellows) for the main keyboard—no tactile bump, no click, just smooth actuation. Keycaps are Cherry profile in an off-white color that doesn't show dust as much as black. The stab bars are band-aid modded so the spacebar and shift keys don't rattle. It's a 75% layout (full arrows, lost the numberpad). Typing on it is smooth and quiet enough to not annoy housemates during late-night coding.

## Why This Setup
I tried clicky switches once—they felt satisfying for the first hour, then got annoying. Tactile switches are in the middle but never feel quite right to me. Linear just works: fast, predictable, quiet. The keycaps took time to find because most cheap options have uneven coloring or wear through quickly. I spent extra on this set because I type for hours every day, and cheap caps start looking sad after a few months. The investment pays back in comfort and not wanting to replace them constantly.
