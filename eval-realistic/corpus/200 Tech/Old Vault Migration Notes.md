# Old Vault Migration Notes

A record of migrating this vault's folder structure from an earlier, messier layout into a cleaner organization.

## Before: The Chaos
The old structure had everything at the top level—personal notes mixed with tech documentation, no clear categorization. Filenames were inconsistent, some using dates as prefixes, others just descriptive names. Searching worked but browsing the vault was overwhelming. After about 600 notes, the disorganization became painful.

## After: The Current Structure
I reorganized into broad categories: 100 Archive (old, read-only), 200 Tech (homelab and systems), 300 Personal (life and non-tech), plus folders for Books and Projects. Within 200 Tech I grouped by domain: Marionette, Larder, infrastructure notes. The personal section is organized by topic. This hierarchy is shallow enough to navigate easily but structured enough to find things. The migration took a weekend of careful moving and link updating, but the resulting clarity was worth it. New notes now have a clear home and maintaining the vault is far less friction.
