# Standing Desk Notes

Height preferences for the standing desk at different times of day based on the type of work being done.

## Sitting Height for Coding
When doing focused coding work, I keep the desk at a comfortable seated height—elbows at 90 degrees, monitors at eye level. This setup feels stable and encourages good posture for deep work. I typically stay seated for 60-90 minutes before switching.

## Standing Height for Reading and Meetings
When reading documentation, attending video calls, or reviewing designs, I raise the desk to standing height. This height works better for side-to-side movement and leaning in to read fine details. Standing keeps energy levels up during calls and makes it easier to pace or stretch. I can comfortably stand for about 45 minutes before my feet ask for a break. Alternating between sitting and standing every hour or so feels like the sweet spot—neither all standing nor all sitting.
