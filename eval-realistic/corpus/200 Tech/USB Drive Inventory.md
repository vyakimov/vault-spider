# USB Drive Inventory

Which USB drives are used for the offline backup rotation and their capacities.

## The Fleet
Two 1TB drives and one 2TB drive rotate in monthly. The 1TB drives get backups A and B (monthly snapshots 2–3 months apart), and the 2TB gets the latest full backup. One drive is always in the drawer, one might be offsite temporarily, one is probably connected to LordByron for the monthly backup process. They're labeled with a paint marker and dates of what's on them.

## Maintenance
Every 6 months I test one drive to make sure it's still readable. So far no failures. They stay in a static-bag in a drawer because USB drives don't really get better by being used. The real insurance is the Blackbird NAS—the USB drives are just the "paranoid extra" copy. If LordByron and Blackbird both died, I'd still have data from 1–2 months ago. Acceptable loss.
