# Reading Highlight Export Notes

A workflow for exporting ebook highlights and annotations into this vault's book-notes folder for future reference.

## Export Process
When I finish an ebook, I export highlights from the reader app as a text file or markdown. This includes the quoted passages and my notes. I then create a new note in the vault named after the book and author, copying the highlights into a structured format: original quotes with page numbers, followed by my thoughts and reactions. Some highlights get tagged with concepts they relate to, creating linkage to other notes in the vault.

## Integration with Reading
This process forces me to review highlights while the book is fresh, reinforcing key ideas. Adding my own notes alongside quotes means the book notes become personal commentary, not just a passive collection. Later when searching for a concept, I often find relevant passages captured from my reading. The workflow takes about 15 minutes per finished book but creates a searchable, personal library of reading insights connected to my other work.
