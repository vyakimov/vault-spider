# Label Printer Notes

Using a small label printer for cables and storage bins.

## The Device
A tiny thermal label printer that cost $35. No ink cartridges (uses heat-sensitive paper), connects via Bluetooth, and prints from the phone or laptop. Labels are about 1.5 inches wide—perfect for cables and small storage bins. The software is basic but functional. Print speed is fast enough for batch-labeling a bunch of cables in 10 minutes.

## What I Label
Cables (both ends to identify purpose quickly), storage bins on shelves (which era's backups, which tools live here), and even the network switch ports (which VLAN is which). The labels are semi-permanent but peel off cleanly if I need to change something. It's a small tool that transformed the desk from chaos into something organized. Not essential, but the ROI is genuinely good because I use it at least weekly. The label roll lasts about 6 months.
