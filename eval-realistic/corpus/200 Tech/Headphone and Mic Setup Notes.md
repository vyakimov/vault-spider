# Headphone and Mic Setup Notes

Audio configuration used for the rare but important video calls and recorded sessions.

## Headphone Setup
I use a decent open-back headphone model that delivers clarity without isolation—important for staying aware of ambient noise. They connect via a headphone amp to keep volume at reasonable levels without distortion. The cable runs through a spiral wrap so it doesn't tangle, and I have a hook on the desk monitor arm to rest them between uses. Closed-back headphones would be better for noise blocking but open-back lets me hear the doorbell or someone calling from another room.

## Microphone Choice
A condenser mic on a boom arm picks up clean audio for calls and occasional recordings. It sits about 6 inches from my mouth, positioned to minimize plosives. A pop filter sits between the mic and my mouth. The mic connects to an audio interface that handles gain and monitoring. The setup is minimal but performs well—remote participants have complimented the audio quality and background noise is minimal. I only use this setup a few times a month, so the cost was spread over years of good video call performance.
