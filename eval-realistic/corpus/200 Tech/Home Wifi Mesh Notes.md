# Home Wifi Mesh Notes

Access point placement after persistent dead spots in one corner of the apartment.

## The Dead Zone
The far bedroom and bathroom got maybe 20% signal strength from the main router. Lots of walls, and the microwave was probably not helping. I tested with a portable access point in different locations and found that the hallway closet (central location, slightly elevated) gave the best coverage. I ran an ethernet cable there and added a second AP.

## Results
Coverage is now solid everywhere. The two APs share the same SSID and band, so devices roam seamlessly. I ran the cable in conduit along the ceiling so it's mostly invisible. The second AP cost $80 and saved me from the "move the router" debate that would have gone in circles for months. The placement is documented with a diagram in this note so I remember why it's in that random closet.
