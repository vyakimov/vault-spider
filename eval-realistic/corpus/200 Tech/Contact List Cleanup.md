# Contact List Cleanup

Deduplicating contacts after years of merges across devices.

## The Problem
I had imported contacts from old phones, merges from Google, and manual adds over years. Result: "John D." appeared 4 times with different phone numbers, some entries had no names, and the list was a mess. Rather than use an auto-dedup tool (which gets decisions wrong), I went through manually and consolidated.

## Process
I sorted by first name and checked for near-duplicates. For ambiguous cases, I kept the entry with the most complete info (email + phone). For people I hadn't contacted in 3+ years, I moved them to an archive list rather than deleting. Now the main contacts list is about 80 people—folks I actually interact with. The archive has another 60 for historical reference. This split helps me focus on current relationships while not completely losing touch information.
