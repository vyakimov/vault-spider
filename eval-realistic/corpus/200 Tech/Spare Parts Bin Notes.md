# Spare Parts Bin Notes

A record of which spare cables, drives, and adapters are worth keeping on hand for quick troubleshooting, versus what's just clutter.

## Worth Keeping
Extra Ethernet cables in various lengths—servers and lab equipment fail at unpredictable times and having CAT6 handy saves a trip to the store. A handful of SATA and USB cables from older devices. Spare RAM sticks and drive enclosures. A couple of blank USB drives for recovery images. Power supplies rated for different voltages since not all electronics use the same barrel connector. These items get pulled out at least once a year.

## Regular Purge
I don't keep old phone chargers or obsolete 30-pin cables anymore—they clutter the bin and I'm unlikely to ever need them. Retired drives get wiped and recycled, not stored. Broken items that I optimistically thought I'd repair get tossed. Every six months I review the bin and remove anything I haven't touched in two years. The goal is a lean, practical inventory of parts that actually solve problems.
