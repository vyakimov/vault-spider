# Backpack Gear List

The everyday-carry pieces I've settled on after realizing I was carrying dead weight.

## Core Kit
My main pack carries a 13-inch laptop, two mechanical pencils (one backup), a small A6 notebook, and a cable roll with USB-C and lightning. A microfiber cloth lives in the front pocket—screens get dusty faster than I expect. I used to carry a full power bank; now I just accept charging is a destination, not a continuous feature.

## Why Each Item Stayed
The notebook gets daily use during code reviews and meetings. The pencils make sense because I write faster than I type sometimes. The cable roll earned its space after the "forgot to charge at a meeting" incident. I dropped the multitool, the notebook stylus set, and the "just in case" USB adapter collection—they were optimism, not habit.
