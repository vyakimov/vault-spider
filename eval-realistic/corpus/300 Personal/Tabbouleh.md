A [[recipe]] for tabbouleh


1 cup bulgur wheat
1.5 cups boiling vegetable stock
3 cups fresh parsley, finely chopped
1 cup fresh mint, finely chopped
4 medium tomatoes, finely diced
1 cucumber, finely diced
1/2 red onion, minced
1/4 cup lemon juice
1/3 cup olive oil
salt and pepper to taste

pour stock over bulgur, let sit 15 minutes until tender and liquid absorbed,
fluff with a fork and let cool, combine parsley mint tomato cucumber and onion,
whisk together lemon juice and oil, toss bulgur with vegetables and dressing,
season with salt and pepper, chill before serving
