What was needed for the last passport renewal, kept for next time.

- Two identical passport photos (4x6cm), printed by the official place not DIY
- Original passport
- Birth certificate (full version, not short form)
- Proof of citizenship (naturalisation certificate in my case)
- Proof of residence (council tax or bank statement, dated within 3 months)
- Application form completed in blue or black pen, no corrections
- Cheque for the full amount (keep receipt)
- Took 4 weeks, not the stated 6, arrived on a Friday

Next renewal probably not until 2031 unless I lose it. Keep this file updated if the process changes.
