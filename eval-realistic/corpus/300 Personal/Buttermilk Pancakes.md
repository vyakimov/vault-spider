A [[recipe]] for buttermilk pancakes


250g all-purpose flour
2 tbsp sugar
2 tsp baking powder
1/2 tsp baking soda
1/2 tsp salt
400ml buttermilk
2 eggs
2 tbsp melted butter
1 tsp vanilla extract

whisk together flour sugar baking powder baking soda and salt in a large bowl,
whisk buttermilk eggs butter and vanilla in another bowl, fold wet into dry just
until combined with some lumps remaining, heat griddle or skillet over medium
heat lightly buttered, pour 1/4 cup batter per pancake, cook until edges look set
and bubbles form on top about 2 minutes, flip and cook until golden another minute,
serve warm with maple syrup and butter
