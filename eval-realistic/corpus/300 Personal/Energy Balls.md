A [[recipe]] for energy balls


200g pitted dates
100g raw almonds
50g unsweetened cocoa powder
2 tbsp almond butter
1 tbsp coconut oil
1 tsp vanilla extract
1/2 tsp sea salt
cocoa powder or unsweetened shredded coconut for coating

pulse dates and almonds in food processor until finely chopped, add cocoa powder
almond butter coconut oil vanilla and salt, pulse until mixture holds together,
scoop into 1-inch balls, roll in cocoa powder or coconut, refrigerate for 30 minutes
before serving, store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to two weeks
