A [[recipe]] for salsa verde


500g tomatillos, husked
2-3 jalapeños, stemmed
1/2 white onion
2 cloves garlic
1/4 cup fresh cilantro
2 tbsp lime juice
1/2 tsp salt

place tomatillos and jalapeños on a baking sheet and broil 8-10 minutes until
charred and soft, transfer to a food processor with onion garlic cilantro and
lime juice, pulse until chunky but not completely smooth, season with salt,
transfer to a serving bowl and let cool, serve at room temperature with chips
or as a condiment
