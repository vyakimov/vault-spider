# Annual Checkup Reminders

A recurring list of health and home maintenance tasks scheduled once per year, triggered by a calendar event in early March.

## Health Reminders
Schedule the annual physical exam and dental cleaning (spring, around March). Get an eye exam if the prescription feels off. Check all medications expiration dates and dispose of old bottles. Review the contents of the first-aid kit and restock bandages and pain relief as needed.

## Home Maintenance Reminders
Test all smoke detector batteries and replace as needed. Inspect the gutters for debris and leaf buildup. Run water through all unused drains to keep them clear. Check the water heater for leaks and corrosion. These tasks prevent small issues from becoming expensive emergency calls.
