A list of prompts used on days when nothing comes to mind.

- What did I avoid doing today and why?
- Who made me laugh recently and what was the moment?
- What would I do with an extra hour tomorrow?
- A decision I regret and what I'd do differently.
- Something small that went right today.
- Who have I been thinking about and why?
- What am I not saying out loud that I need to?
- What are three things I'm grateful for that I took for granted?
- If I could change one thing about last week, what would it be?
- What does my ideal day look like, realistically?
- A recurring thought I've had and whether I believe it.
- What scared me recently and did the fear match reality?
- One thing I'm learning about myself right now.
- What does self-care actually look like to me, not what I think it should?
- A conversation I wish I could have.

**Notes:** don't force it if nothing wants to come out, some days journaling is just three sentences, not all prompts fit every mood, I've been doing this for a year and it's mostly therapeutic rather than profound, rereading old entries is sometimes cringe, sometimes useful.
