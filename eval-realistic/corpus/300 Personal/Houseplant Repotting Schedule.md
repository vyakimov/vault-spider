# Houseplant Repotting Schedule

A tracking list of which plants have outgrown their pots and when to move them up to the next size.

## Current Queue
- **Monstera** in the corner needs a 2-inch bump; roots are circling. Plan to do this in late July.
- **Pothos** on the shelf is stunted—roots bound since spring. High priority.
- **Snake plant** can wait another season; it likes being pot-bound.

## Potting Supplies and Notes
Keep a small stock of 6, 8, and 10-inch nursery pots on the shelf. Cactus mix for the succulents, general potting soil for the others. Always use pots with drainage holes and water thoroughly after repotting, then hold off for a week to let the roots settle.
