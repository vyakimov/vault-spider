# Houseplant Light Levels

Which rooms get enough light for which plants, learned the hard way.

## Light by Room
The south-facing bedroom gets strong direct sun until 2 PM—good for succulents but burns delicate foliage. The living room has bright indirect light all day. The north-facing bathroom has low light, suitable only for shade plants like pothos. The east-facing kitchen gets morning sun.

## Mistakes
I initially put a monstera in the bathroom and watched it yellow over months. Now it lives in the living room and thrives. Learned that "shade plant" doesn't mean "no light"—even shade plants need a few hundred lux.
