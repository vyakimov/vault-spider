Personal shortcut settings for the camera used on trips.

**Sunny day (outdoors):**
- ISO 100, f/5.6, shutter 1/500
- Focus mode: continuous, faces prioritised if available

**Overcast/cloudy:**
- ISO 200–400, f/2.8–4, shutter 1/250
- Manual focus slightly more reliable when light is flat

**Indoor (ambient light, no flash):**
- ISO 1600–3200, f/1.8–2.8, shutter 1/60 (hold steady)
- Shoot RAW for editing latitude

**Sunset/golden hour:**
- ISO 200–400, f/2.8, shutter 1/125
- Expose for the sky slightly, foreground gets warm light anyway

**Fast-moving subjects (kids, animals):**
- ISO 400+, f/4, shutter 1/1000 minimum
- Continuous shooting, burst mode, pick the sharpest frame later

**General:** always check focus before releasing, white balance on auto most of the time, I adjust it in editing if needed, expose to the right in the histogram (sensor forgives blown light less than blown shadows), bracket 3 exposures when unsure.
