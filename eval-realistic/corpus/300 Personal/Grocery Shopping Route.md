# Grocery Shopping Route

The usual shop order that keeps frozen items cold longest.

## The Sequence
I enter at the back where produce and dairy are, do those first (need time to adjust to temp). Then move to pantry staples and dry goods along the outer aisles. Frozen section is second-to-last—those items get picked up just before the checkout. Finally, I grab the few last-minute things (bread, eggs, maybe something else) at the very end. Checkout is quick. From freezer to my car is maybe 3 minutes.

## Why It Matters
If I did frozen first and then spent 20 minutes browsing, the frozen stuff thaws during the trip. This order keeps frozen items in the coldest part of the store until the very end. The trip takes about 35 minutes total. It's a small optimization, but combined with having a list before I go, I'm in and out without impulse buys or the frozen chicken thawing in the car. Simple but it works.
