# Local Market Notes

A quick reference for which vendor stalls at the Saturday market are worth visiting versus which ones have stale stock or inflated prices.

## Best Stalls
The produce vendor near the entrance at stall 14 has fresh greens and tomatoes, always busy but worth the wait. The cheese maker at 8 sells blocks at fair prices and lets me sample before buying—the aged cheddar is consistently good. The bread booth appears around 10 AM and sells out by noon, so I arrive early if I want the sourdough.

## Avoid or Skip
The flower vendor has premium-priced arrangements but the stems wilt within days. The prepared foods stall has inconsistent quality week to week. The coffee stand uses a chain brand that I can get anywhere—the independent roasters come only on the third Saturday of the month.
