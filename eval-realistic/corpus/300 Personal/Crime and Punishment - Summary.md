# Crime and Punishment — Summary

[[book notes]] on Dostoevsky's *Crime and Punishment*. The cast is in
[[Crime and Punishment - Name Glossary]].

## Part 1

Raskolnikov, an impoverished student in St. Petersburg, retreats from society after abandoning his law studies. He lives in a cramped room, isolated and ill, developing a nihilistic philosophy that extraordinary individuals can transgress moral law for higher purposes. He decides to murder an elderly pawnbroker to steal her money and prove his theory.

## Part 2

Raskolnikov murders the pawnbroker and her sister Lizaveta in a panic. Afterward, wracked with fever and guilt, he retreats to his room. The detective Porfiry begins investigating the murders. Raskolnikov meets Sonya, the daughter of a destitute widow, who has been forced to become a prostitute to support her family. Their meeting plants seeds of moral awakening.

## Part 3

Raskolnikov confesses to the murders, though he struggles with pride and resentment even as he does so. Sonya convinces him that confession and redemption through suffering is the path to salvation. He accepts his guilt, not as a criminal but as a man who has violated his own humanity.

## Part 4

Raskolnikov is sentenced to hard labor in Siberia. Sonya follows him there. The novel ends with Raskolnikov in prison, beginning to contemplate spiritual rebirth through Sonya's Christian faith and the possibility of redemption through love and suffering.
