A [[recipe]] for miso soup

1L dashi stock
3 tbsp white miso paste
200g silken tofu, cubed
2 spring onions, sliced
nori (seaweed), torn into pieces
optional: mushrooms, wakame seaweed, clams

heat dashi until steaming but not boiling, spoon miso into a small bowl and whisk with a few tablespoons of the hot dashi to dissolve it smoothly, pour the miso mixture back into the pot, add tofu cubes and any harder vegetables like mushrooms, simmer very gently for 2-3 minutes (don't boil as this damages the miso's probiotics and flavour), pour into bowls, top with spring onions and nori, serve immediately
