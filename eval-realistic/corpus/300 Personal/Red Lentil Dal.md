A [[recipe]] for red lentil dal


2 tbsp ghee or oil
1 onion, diced
4 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced
1 tsp turmeric
1 tsp ground cumin
1/2 tsp ground coriander
1/4 tsp cayenne
250g red lentils, rinsed
4 cups vegetable stock
1 can (400ml) coconut milk
salt to taste
fresh cilantro for serving

heat ghee, sauté onion until golden, add garlic ginger and spices, cook 1 minute
until fragrant, add lentils and stock, bring to boil then simmer 20 minutes until
lentils break down, stir in coconut milk, season with salt, cook 5 more minutes,
serve with cilantro and flatbread
