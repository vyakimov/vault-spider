Informal notes on what helps or hurts sleep quality.

**Helps:**
- Running or exercise in the afternoon (but not within 4 hours of bed)
- Herbal tea, specifically chamomile or passionflower, sipped 30 min before bed
- Reading something light, absolutely not email or news
- Consistent bedtime, even on weekends (boring but effective)
- No screens after 10:30pm (the blue light thing is real)
- Blackout blinds, white noise from a fan
- Cool room, around 18°C seems ideal for me
- Journaling anxieties before sleep, getting them out of my head

**Hurts:**
- Coffee after 2pm (affects me for 12+ hours, didn't realise at first)
- Alcohol, especially beer — dehydrating and disrupts REM sleep
- Eating heavy meals after 7pm
- Worrying about sleep itself (meta but true)
- News before bed, doomscroll spiral
- Napping, even 20 minutes wrecks nighttime sleep

**Current routine:** bed by 11pm, wake naturally around 7am, getting 7–8 hours most nights now.
