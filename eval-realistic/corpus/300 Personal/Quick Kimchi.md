A [[recipe]] for quick kimchi

1 napa cabbage (about 1kg), cut into 5cm pieces
3 tbsp sea salt
6 garlic cloves, minced
2 tbsp fresh ginger, minced
3 tbsp gochugaru (Korean red chilli flakes)
2 tbsp fish sauce
1 tbsp sugar
3 spring onions, sliced
1 tsp sesame oil

dissolve salt in 1L of cold water, soak cabbage for 2 hours, drain well and squeeze out excess liquid, combine garlic, ginger, gochugaru, fish sauce and sugar in a bowl, toss through the drained cabbage with spring onions and sesame oil, pack into a jar, press down so the liquid rises above the vegetables, leave at room temperature for 2-3 days (covered loosely) before chilling, it will develop tanginess over time
