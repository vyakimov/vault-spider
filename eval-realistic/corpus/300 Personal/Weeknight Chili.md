A [[recipe]] for weeknight chili


2 tbsp olive oil
1 large onion, diced
4 cloves garlic, minced
500g ground beef
2 cans (400g each) kidney beans, drained and rinsed
1 can (800g) crushed tomatoes
2 tbsp tomato paste
2 tsp ground cumin
1 tsp chili powder
1/2 tsp cayenne (optional)
1 tsp oregano
salt and pepper to taste
1 cup beef stock

heat oil, sauté onion and garlic until fragrant, brown meat breaking it up as it cooks,
stir in tomato paste and spices, cook 1 minute, add tomatoes beans and stock, simmer
25-30 minutes until thickened and flavors meld, season to taste
