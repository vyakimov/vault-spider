A [[recipe]] for minestrone soup

2 tbsp olive oil
1 onion, diced
2 carrots, chopped
2 celery stalks, chopped
3 garlic cloves, minced
1 can diced tomatoes (400g)
1L vegetable stock
400g tin white beans, drained
150g green beans, cut into 2cm pieces
100g pasta (small shapes like ditalini)
handful fresh parsley, chopped
1 bay leaf
salt and black pepper to taste

heat oil, soften the onion/carrot/celery base for 5 min, add garlic until fragrant, pour in stock and tomatoes with their juice, add bay leaf and simmer 15 min, stir in beans and green beans, cook 10 min more, add pasta and simmer until just tender (usually 8-10 min), finish with parsley and season to taste
