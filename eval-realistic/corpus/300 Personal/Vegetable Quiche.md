A [[recipe]] for vegetable quiche


1 pie crust (thawed if frozen)
2 tbsp olive oil
1 onion, diced
2 cloves garlic, minced
200g broccoli florets, chopped small
150g mushrooms, sliced
100g bell pepper, diced
1 cup shredded Cheddar cheese
6 eggs
1 cup heavy cream
1/2 tsp salt
1/4 tsp black pepper
1/4 tsp nutmeg

preheat oven to 375°F, place crust in a 9-inch pie dish, sauté onion garlic
broccoli mushrooms and pepper in oil until tender, scatter vegetables and cheese
in crust, whisk eggs with cream and seasonings, pour over vegetables, bake 35-40
minutes until filling is set but still slightly jiggly in center, cool 10 minutes
before serving
