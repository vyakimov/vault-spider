# Home Budget Categories

How monthly spending gets bucketed for the budget notes, and what counts as discretionary.

## Categories
I track 9 main categories: housing (rent, utilities, maintenance), groceries, transportation, dining out, entertainment, subscriptions, medical, insurance, and clothing. Each category has a monthly budget target based on historical spending.

## Rules
Discretionary includes entertainment, dining out, and non-essential clothing. Everything else is fixed/semi-fixed. I allow 10% overage in any category per month, but quarterly totals must not exceed budget. Unused budget doesn't roll over—this keeps spending predictable.
