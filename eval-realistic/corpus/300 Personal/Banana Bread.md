A [[recipe]] for banana bread


3 overripe bananas, mashed
250g all-purpose flour
200g sugar
100g butter, softened
2 eggs
1 tsp vanilla extract
1 tsp baking soda
1/2 tsp salt
1/2 tsp cinnamon
1/4 tsp nutmeg

cream butter and sugar until light, beat in eggs one at a time, add vanilla,
fold in mashed banana, mix flour with baking soda salt and spices, gently fold
into wet ingredients until just combined, pour into greased loaf tin, bake at
350°F for 50-60 minutes until a skewer comes out clean
