A short home yoga routine and which poses to skip on a bad back day.

**Standard 20-minute routine (5x a week):**
1. Child's pose (1 min)
2. Cat-cow stretches (1 min, 10 rounds)
3. Downward dog (30 sec)
4. Sun salutation A (3 rounds, 2 min)
5. Warrior I, II, reverse warrior flow (2 min each side)
6. Triangle pose (30 sec each side)
7. Tree pose, balance work (1 min)
8. Seated forward fold (1 min)
9. Pigeon pose (1 min each side)
10. Reclined twist (1 min each side)
11. Savasana/corpse pose (5 min)

**On bad back days, skip:**
- Downward dog (too much strain)
- Full sun salutations (replace with gentle cat-cow)
- Pigeon pose (hip opener can aggravate lower back)

**Replace with:**
- Reclined figure-four (easier on the spine than pigeon)
- Gentle spinal twists, knees to one side
- Supported bridge pose (less intense than full backbend)

**Notes:** morning practice feels better than evening, consistency matters more than intensity, if something pinches (not just stretches), back off immediately, I'm not training for anything, just trying to stay mobile and calm.
