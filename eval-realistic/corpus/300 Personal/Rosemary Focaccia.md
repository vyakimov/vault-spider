A [[recipe]] for rosemary focaccia


500g bread flour
300ml warm water
7g instant yeast
2 tsp salt
5 tbsp olive oil, divided
2 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
sea salt for topping
cracked black pepper

mix flour yeast and salt, add water and 3 tbsp oil, knead until smooth 8-10 minutes,
let rise in oiled bowl until doubled about 1 hour, turn dough into oiled 9x13 inch
pan and let rise 30 minutes, dimple surface gently with fingertips, drizzle with
remaining oil, scatter rosemary sea salt and pepper, bake at 425°F for 20-25 minutes
until golden, cool on wire rack before serving
