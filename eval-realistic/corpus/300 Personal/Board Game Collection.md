Board games owned and who tends to win.

**7 Wonders** — Alex dominates if it's their second play, otherwise it's chaos
**Ticket to Ride** — Morgan always locks down one transcontinental route early and cashes out, usually wins
**Catan** — balanced but Jordan has eerie luck with rolls, Casey protests the unfairness
**Splendor** — short game, good for coffee afternoons, everyone takes turns winning
**Codenames** — word association chaos, no clear winner, just fun arguments
**Agricola** — deep strategy, takes 2 hours, I tend to win because I over-plan, friends hate that
**Pandemic** — cooperative, we've beaten it twice, lost spectacularly three times, good for talking through decisions
**Azul** — quick, beautiful pieces, deceptively tactical, usually a close game

Games most likely to be packed for trips: Splendor and Codenames (quick, small boxes). Games that don't come out often: Agricola (exhausting) and Ticket to Ride (Casey refuses to play because they lost badly once).
