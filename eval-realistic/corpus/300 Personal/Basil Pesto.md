A [[recipe]] for basil pesto


100g fresh basil leaves, loosely packed
50g Parmesan cheese, grated
50g pine nuts
3 cloves garlic
1/3 cup olive oil
2 tbsp lemon juice
salt and pepper to taste

combine basil pine nuts garlic and Parmesan in a food processor, pulse until
coarsely chopped, slowly drizzle olive oil while pulsing to achieve desired
consistency, add lemon juice and pulse to combine, season with salt and pepper,
use immediately or store in an airtight container with plastic wrap pressed
directly on surface to prevent browning, refrigerate up to one week
