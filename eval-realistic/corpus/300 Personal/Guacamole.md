A [[recipe]] for guacamole


3 ripe avocados
1 lime, juiced
1/2 tsp salt
1/2 red onion, minced
2 jalapeños, minced
1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
2 medium tomatoes, diced
pinch of black pepper

halve avocados remove pit and scoop flesh into a bowl, immediately toss with lime
juice and salt, gently fold in onion jalapeños and cilantro, stir in tomato, season
with pepper to taste, serve with tortilla chips, pressing plastic wrap directly onto
surface to prevent browning if making ahead
