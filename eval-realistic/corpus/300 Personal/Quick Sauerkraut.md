A [[recipe]] for quick sauerkraut

1 head green cabbage (about 1.2kg), finely shredded
1.5 tbsp sea salt
1 tbsp caraway seeds, optional
1 tsp juniper berries, optional

combine shredded cabbage and salt in a large bowl, massage vigorously for 10 minutes until the cabbage releases its liquid and wilts down significantly, add caraway seeds and juniper berries if using, pack tightly into a clean jar, pressing down so the brine rises above the vegetables, weight it down with a smaller jar or fermentation weight so everything stays submerged, cover loosely or use an airlock lid, leave at room temperature for 3-5 days (taste from day 3 onwards), once sour to your liking, refrigerate to slow fermentation
