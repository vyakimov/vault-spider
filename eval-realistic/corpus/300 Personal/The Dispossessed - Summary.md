## Anarres 1: Departure

Shevek, a brilliant physicist on the anarchist moon colony Anarres, decides to travel to the capitalist world of Urras to share his unified theory of time and overcome the intellectual isolation of his world. Though Anarres is built on principles of non-possession and equality, it has become bureaucratic and stifling. Shevek's departure is controversial—some see him as a traitor, others as a necessary bridge.

## Urras 1: Arrival and Disorientation

Shevek arrives on Urras, a world of hierarchy, wealth, and beauty. He is hosted by A-Io, a wealthy capitalist nation that wants to control his physics. Gradually, Shevek becomes aware of class divides, poverty, and violence beneath Urras's civilized surface. He has access to comfort and libraries but feels increasingly trapped by political machinations.

## Anarres 2: Return and Conflict

Intercut chapters show deterioration on Anarres: drought, food shortages, and increased authoritarianism from the Syndicate of Initiators. Shevek's partner Takver struggles to maintain their relationship and family across his absence, and the colony itself faces existential crisis as factionalism grows.

## Urras 2: Revolution and Return

Shevek becomes involved in revolutionary politics on Urras, refuses to be used by any power, and decides to return to Anarres. His escape with the help of an underground movement parallels the intellectual and political awakening he undergoes. He returns home changed but committed to his people, carrying the knowledge that both worlds are flawed but that genuine human connection and the struggle for freedom matter more than ideology.
