A [[recipe]] for ratatouille


3 tbsp olive oil
1 onion, diced
4 cloves garlic, minced
1 bell pepper, diced
1 small eggplant, diced
2 zucchini, diced
3 medium tomatoes, diced
1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
2 tbsp tomato paste
1 tsp dried oregano
1 tsp dried basil
1/2 tsp dried thyme
salt and pepper to taste
fresh basil for serving

heat oil in large pot, sauté onion until soft 5 minutes, add garlic and cook 30 seconds,
add bell pepper eggplant and zucchini, cook 10 minutes stirring occasionally, stir in
fresh and canned tomatoes tomato paste and dried herbs, simmer 30-40 minutes until
vegetables are very tender and flavors meld, season with salt and pepper, serve warm
or at room temperature garnished with fresh basil
