A [[recipe]] for hummus


1 can (400g) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
3 tbsp tahini
3 tbsp lemon juice
2 cloves garlic, minced
3 tbsp ice water
2 tbsp olive oil
1/2 tsp ground cumin
salt to taste
paprika for serving

place chickpeas garlic tahini and lemon juice in a food processor, blend until
creamy, add water a tablespoon at a time to reach desired consistency, drizzle in
olive oil while blending, season with salt and cumin, serve drizzled with olive
oil and sprinkled with paprika
