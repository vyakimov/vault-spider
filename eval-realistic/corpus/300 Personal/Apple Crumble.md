A [[recipe]] for apple crumble

For the filling:
1kg cooking apples, peeled, cored and sliced
50g caster sugar
1 tsp vanilla extract
1/2 tsp cinnamon
1 tbsp lemon juice

For the crumble topping:
150g plain flour
100g cold butter, cut into cubes
100g rolled oats
75g caster sugar
pinch of salt

toss apples with sugar, vanilla, cinnamon and lemon juice, spread evenly in a baking dish, rub flour and cold butter together until breadcrumbs, stir through oats, sugar and salt, scatter evenly over the apples without pressing down, bake at 180C for 35-40 minutes until the top is golden and the apples are tender when tested with a fork, serve warm with custard or ice cream
