# Coffee Grinder Notes

The dial settings that actually work for each brew method I use at home.

## Dial Settings by Method
French press needs a coarse setting around 8–9 (big pieces, less extraction). Moka pot likes 4–5, which is medium-fine; going coarser than that gives weak, watery shots. Pour-over sits at 6–7 depending on how long I pour. I've stopped chasing the "perfect" setting—consistency in timing matters more than the exact grind size, within reason.

## What Changed
I switched to this grinder from a blade grinder three months ago. The blade stuff was chaotic: sometimes dust, sometimes chunks. Now I get repeatable results, which means I can actually taste when the beans are stale versus when I'm just doing the method wrong. The dial moves with some friction, so I've marked the common settings with a thin paint pen on the hopper.
