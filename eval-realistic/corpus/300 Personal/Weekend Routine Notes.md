# Weekend Routine Notes

A loose weekend routine that keeps chores from piling up.

## Saturday Morning
I spend the first hour of Saturday morning doing laundry and a quick tidy: dishes, wipe down kitchen surfaces, and deal with whatever pile of stuff accumulated during the week. The rest of the day is free. By keeping Saturday morning predictable, the weekend actually starts relaxed instead of me staring at mess.

## Sunday Prep
Sunday evening is when I plan the week: check the calendar, prep any meals that store well, lay out the gym clothes, and make a small list of what needs doing Monday. It takes about 30 minutes and completely eliminates the Sunday-evening doom feeling. The routine is intentionally loose—if something is more urgent on Saturday, the routine shifts. But having this default keeps me from spiraling into "I'll deal with it later."
