# Morning Routine Notes

What actually happens most mornings versus the idealized version.

## The Reality
I wake up, make coffee, check messages (I said I wouldn't, but I do), and sit for 20 minutes with the coffee and a book or notebook. That's it. No cold shower, no 5am workout, none of the stuff I convinced myself I'd do. The coffee is non-negotiable though—if I skip that, the morning spirals.

## The Win
The 20-minute buffer before doing anything else sets a better tone than jumping straight into work. I'm not a morning person by nature, so fighting that is pointless. The routine accommodates who I actually am, not who I wish I was. On weekdays I need to be at work by 9, so I need to start the routine by 8 at the latest. Knowing that deadline, everything else fits. It's simple, repeatable, and actually sustainable.
