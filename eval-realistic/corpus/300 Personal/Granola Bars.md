A [[recipe]] for granola bars


300g old-fashioned oats
100g honey or maple syrup
100g almond or peanut butter
100g melted coconut oil
150g mixed dried fruit and nuts (raisins, almonds, coconut flakes)
1 tsp vanilla extract
1/2 tsp salt
1 tsp ground cinnamon

mix honey nut butter and coconut oil together until smooth, stir in oats dried
fruit nuts vanilla salt and cinnamon until well combined, press firmly into a
parchment-lined 8x8 inch pan, bake at 325°F for 18-22 minutes until golden brown,
cool in pan for 15 minutes then cut into bars, cool completely before serving
