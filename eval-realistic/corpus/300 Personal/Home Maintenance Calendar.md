# Home Maintenance Calendar

Seasonal home maintenance tasks (filters, gutters, smoke detectors).

## Schedule
Spring (March-April): Check gutters, test outdoor AC unit, inspect foundation for cracks. Summer (June-July): No major tasks, just watch for roof leaks during storms. Fall (September-October): Clean gutters again, replace HVAC filters, test heating, inspect chimney. Winter (January): Check weatherstripping, test heating system, replace water heater anode if needed.

## Tracking
I keep a simple checklist in Obsidian and check items off as they're completed. Some items recur quarterly (filter changes), others annually (roof inspection). Staying ahead on these prevents expensive emergency repairs.
