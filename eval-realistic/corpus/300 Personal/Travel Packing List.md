A reusable packing list for short trips.

**Toiletries & essentials:**
- Toothbrush, toothpaste, floss
- Deodorant, razor (or travel size)
- Moisturiser, sunscreen
- Medications (painkillers, antacids, antihistamine)
- Glasses/contact lenses if needed
- Phone charger and cable

**Clothes (3-5 nights):**
- 4-5 tops (one nicer for dinners out)
- 2 pairs of trousers/bottoms
- Underwear x5, socks x3-4
- Comfortable shoes, one dressier option
- Light layer (cardigan or fleece)
- Pyjamas, shower shoes

**Miscellaneous:**
- Passport, wallet, keys
- Portable charger
- Pen, small notebook
- Reusable water bottle
- Any booking confirmations (on phone)

**Notes:** pack items I actually enjoy wearing, not obligatory comfortable clothes; check weather before packing layers; leave room in luggage for souvenirs/shopping
