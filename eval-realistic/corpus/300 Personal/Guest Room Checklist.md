# Guest Room Checklist

What to prep before a guest stays over.

## The Setup
I check the sheets a few days prior (wash if needed), fluff the pillows, and make sure there are two extra blankets in the closet. I clean the room's surfaces, vacuum, and leave a small lamp on the nightstand with water and a charging cable. The wastebasket gets emptied. Bathroom gets fresh towels, and I make sure there's basic soap and a clean mirror.

## The Extras
If the guest is staying more than one night, I put a small welcome note with wifi password and any house rules (quiet hours, thermostat adjustments, which shelves in the fridge are theirs). I also clear a drawer or two in the dresser. It takes maybe an hour total, done piecemeal over a few days. The goal is: when they arrive, everything is ready and they can just unpack and settle in. Small touches like fresh flowers or a spare phone charger make a difference.
