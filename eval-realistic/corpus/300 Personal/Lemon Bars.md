A [[recipe]] for lemon bars

For the base:
150g plain flour
50g icing sugar
100g cold butter, cubed
pinch of salt

For the filling:
3 eggs
200g caster sugar
2 tbsp plain flour
1 tsp baking powder
zest of 2 lemons
100ml fresh lemon juice
1 tbsp icing sugar for dusting

mix flour, icing sugar and salt, rub in cold butter until breadcrumbs, press into a greased 20cm square baking tin, bake at 180C for 12-15 minutes until pale golden, whisk eggs and sugar together until pale, fold through flour, baking powder, lemon zest and juice, pour over the base, bake at 180C for 20-25 minutes until set but still slightly wobbly in the centre, cool completely, dust with icing sugar and cut into squares
