A [[recipe]] for gazpacho

1kg ripe tomatoes, rough chunks
1 red bell pepper, seeded and chopped
1/2 cucumber, peeled and chopped
2 garlic cloves, minced
50ml red wine vinegar
60ml extra virgin olive oil
250ml tomato juice or water
1 tsp cumin
salt and black pepper to taste
handful stale bread, optional (helps thicken)

blend tomatoes, pepper, cucumber and garlic until mostly smooth (leave some texture), whisk in vinegar and olive oil, thin with tomato juice or water to desired consistency, season with cumin, salt and pepper, chill for at least 2 hours before serving, taste and adjust seasoning as needed
