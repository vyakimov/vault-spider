A [[recipe]] for baba ganoush

2 large aubergines (about 900g total)
4 garlic cloves, minced
60ml tahini
3 tbsp lemon juice
2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
salt to taste
pinch of cumin
paprika for garnish
parsley for garnish

char whole aubergines over a flame or under the grill until blackened all over and soft inside (about 20 min), scoop flesh into a bowl, mash well to break up any lumps, fold in garlic, tahini, lemon juice and olive oil, season with salt and cumin, taste and adjust, transfer to a serving bowl, drizzle with more olive oil, dust with paprika and scatter parsley on top
