A [[recipe]] for sheet pan roasted vegetables


500g broccoli florets
400g Brussels sprouts, halved
300g carrots, cut into 2-inch pieces
300g bell peppers, cut into chunks
200g red onion, cut into wedges
4 tbsp olive oil
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 tsp dried Italian herbs
salt and pepper to taste
grated Parmesan (optional)

toss vegetables with olive oil garlic herbs salt and pepper, spread in a single
layer on a baking sheet, roast at 425°F for 30-35 minutes, stirring halfway through,
until golden and tender, finish with Parmesan if desired, serve hot or at room
temperature
