Which cuttings rooted successfully and which didn't.

**Rooted successfully (3+ weeks):**
- Pothos cuttings in water (started July, 8/10 sprouted roots by August)
- Monstera nodes in perlite (started June, 6/8 rooted, slower than water)
- Coleus in water (started August, fast root development, propagates almost aggressively)
- Begonia leaf cuttings in peat (started July, 3/5 developed new growth)

**Failed or still waiting (6+ weeks, probably dead):**
- Peperomia stem cuttings (tried perlite, likely too wet)
- Sansevieria leaf cuttings (tried water, one rotted immediately, others never did anything)
- Alocasia cutting (maybe the plant was stressed to begin with)

**Next to try:**
- Succulents in dry perlite (I keep overwatering them)
- Tradescantia cuttings (everyone says they're foolproof, want to prove it)
- Philodendron cuttings in sphagnum (heard this works better than water)

**Lessons:** water propagation is faster and harder to kill, but perlite/soil gives hardier plants in the end, always take more cuttings than you think you need (50% success is normal), don't overwater propagation medium especially soil-based, patience, give it time.
