Cast list: Dorothea, Casaubon, Lydgate, Rosamond, and the rest.

## Central Characters

**Dorothea Brooke** — The heroine, an idealistic young woman with intellectual aspirations in a provincial setting. Marries the scholar Casaubon for intellectual partnership, only to discover his work is hollow. Eventually marries Will Ladislaw, choosing love over social advantage.

**Edward Casaubon** — A pedantic elderly scholar working on a massive, ultimately futile theological manuscript. Cold, insecure, and unable to respond to Dorothea's emotional needs. His death early in the second half frees Dorothea from his restrictions.

**Tertius Lydgate** — An idealistic young doctor committed to medical reform and scientific discovery. Brilliant and ambitious, but gradually compromised by marriage to Rosamond and by financial difficulties. Represents the tragedy of unrealized intellectual potential.

**Rosamond Vincy** — Beautiful, accomplished in superficial ways (music, drawing), and conventionally ambitious. She has no interest in Lydgate's scientific work and instead focuses on social status and domestic comfort, becoming a force that undermines his ideals.

**Will Ladislaw** — Casaubon's young cousin, artistic and free-spirited, who comes to Middlemarch as Casaubon's assistant. Falls in love with Dorothea and marries her, choosing a political career and modest life with her over wealth.

## Secondary Characters

**James Chettam** — A conventional gentleman farmer who marries Celia, Dorothea's sister. Represents provincial respectability and contentment with limited horizons.

**Celia Brooke** — Dorothea's younger sister, practical and domestic rather than idealistic; she is happy with Chettam and provides a foil to Dorothea's intellectual intensity.

**Fred Vincy** — Rosamond's brother, irresponsible and gambling-prone in youth; he matures and marries Mary Garth, finding contentment in modest circumstances.

**Mary Garth** — Intelligent and principled, she loves Fred and represents the possibility of intellectual partnership in humble circumstances.
