Cast list: Shevek, Takver, Sabul, and the rest.

## Central Characters

**Shevek** — A brilliant theoretical physicist from Anarres, the anarchist moon colony. Idealistic but increasingly disillusioned with Anarres's stagnation, he travels to Urras seeking intellectual freedom and the opportunity to share his breakthrough in unified temporal theory. Represents the tension between individual ambition and collective responsibility.

**Takver** — Shevek's partner and lover, a talented biologist on Anarres who is left behind when he travels to Urras. Patient and steadfast, she waits for him while struggling to maintain their relationship across vast distance and to defend him against political accusation. Their bond is one of the novel's emotional cores.

**Sabul** — The powerful physicist on Anarres who controls access to research and has stifled Shevek's work. Represents how even anarchist societies can develop hierarchies and oppression, using consensus and ideology to mask tyranny.

**Odo** — The long-dead founder of Anarres's anarchist experiment, revered but increasingly criticized as the colony faces real-world contradictions her philosophy didn't anticipate.

## Secondary Characters

**Voe** — A physicist on Urras from A-Io who becomes Shevek's primary contact and attempts to use him for national advantage.

**Efor** — A revolutionary on Urras who helps Shevek understand the suffering of the underclass and assists in his eventual escape.

**Remethe** — A bureaucrat on Anarres who represents the authoritarian tendency within anarchism.

**Bedap** — Shevek's friend from his youth on Anarres, more integrated into the society than Shevek but also questioning the status quo.
