# Book Club Discussion Notes Format

A lightweight template I've used the last few months to organize thoughts before meetings.

## Section Template
I open with a one-line summary, then bullet my three strongest reactions (scene, character, pacing—whatever stuck). Then I leave a gap for the discussion itself: what others said that surprised me. Finally, I note one thing I'm curious about from the book that nobody mentioned yet. This takes maybe five minutes to fill before the meeting.

## What Worked
Not trying to capture everything—just my own entry points. The "what surprised me" section forces me to actually listen during the meeting instead of rehearsing my prepared thoughts. I've experimented with rating the book, but that always feels reductive. The curiosity question has sparked the best side conversations.
