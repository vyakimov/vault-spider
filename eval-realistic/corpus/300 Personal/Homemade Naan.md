A [[recipe]] for homemade naan


500g all-purpose flour
1 tsp instant yeast
1 tsp salt
1 tbsp sugar
120ml yogurt
60ml warm water
2 tbsp olive oil
nigella seeds or sesame seeds for topping (optional)
butter for brushing

mix flour yeast salt and sugar, add yogurt water and 1 tbsp oil, knead until smooth,
place in oiled bowl and let rise 2-3 hours until doubled, divide into 8 pieces and
roll each into an oval, heat a cast iron skillet or griddle over medium-high heat,
brush with oil and cook naans 2-3 minutes per side until puffed and speckled, brush
with butter and sprinkle seeds if desired, serve warm
