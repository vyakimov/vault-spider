A [[recipe]] for frittata


2 tbsp olive oil
1 onion, diced
200g potatoes, diced small
150g spinach, roughly chopped
100g mushrooms, sliced
8 eggs
1/4 cup milk
100g shredded cheese
1/2 tsp salt
1/4 tsp black pepper
fresh herbs (optional)

preheat oven to 375°F, sauté onion potatoes and mushrooms in an oven-safe skillet
until tender about 8 minutes, add spinach and cook until wilted, whisk together
eggs milk cheese salt and pepper, pour over vegetables, cook on stovetop for 2-3
minutes until edges begin to set, transfer to oven and bake 12-15 minutes until
center is just set, garnish with fresh herbs and serve warm
