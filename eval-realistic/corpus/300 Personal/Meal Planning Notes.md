# Meal Planning Notes

A rough weekly meal-planning routine built around what's in Larder.

## Process
Every Sunday, I check what proteins and produce are in the pantry, then sketch out 4-5 dinners for the week. I avoid buying new ingredients unless I'm running out of staples—this keeps grocery bills low and reduces waste.

## Themes
I usually plan Monday-Wednesday as leftovers/simple meals, Thursday as a more involved dish, Friday as takeout or guest-friendly meal, weekend breakfast-focused. This routine developed over time and seems to work well with my schedule and shopping habits.
