A [[recipe]] for tzatziki

500g Greek yogurt
1 cucumber, peeled and grated
3 garlic cloves, minced very fine
2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped (or 1 tsp dried)
1 tbsp lemon juice
1 tbsp white wine vinegar
2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
salt and black pepper to taste

grate cucumber and squeeze out excess moisture in a clean cloth, fold cucumber into yogurt with garlic, dill, lemon juice, vinegar and olive oil, season generously with salt and pepper, refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving to let flavours meld, taste and adjust seasoning if needed
