A [[recipe]] for fudgy brownies


140g dark chocolate, chopped
115g butter
200g sugar
2 eggs
1 tsp vanilla extract
80g all-purpose flour
30g cocoa powder
1/4 tsp baking powder
1/2 tsp salt

preheat oven to 350°F, melt chocolate and butter together over low heat or in
microwave, stir in sugar and let cool slightly, whisk in eggs one at a time then
vanilla, fold in flour cocoa powder baking powder and salt until just combined,
pour into a greased 8x8 inch pan, bake 20-25 minutes until a toothpick inserted
comes out with just a few crumbs still attached not clean, cool completely before
cutting into squares
