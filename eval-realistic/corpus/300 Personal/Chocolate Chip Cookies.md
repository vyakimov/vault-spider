A [[recipe]] for chocolate chip cookies


225g butter, softened
200g brown sugar
100g white sugar
2 eggs
1 tsp vanilla extract
280g all-purpose flour
1 tsp baking soda
1/2 tsp salt
300g chocolate chips

cream butter and sugars together until light and fluffy 2-3 minutes, beat in eggs
one at a time then vanilla, whisk flour baking soda and salt together, fold into
wet ingredients until just combined, stir in chocolate chips, drop rounded tablespoons
onto baking sheets, bake at 375°F for 9-11 minutes until edges are golden but centers
still soft, cool on baking sheets for 2 minutes then transfer to wire racks
