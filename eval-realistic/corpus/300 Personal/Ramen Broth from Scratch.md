A [[recipe]] for ramen broth from scratch


1.5kg chicken bones (or pork), rinsed
3L water
1 onion, halved and charred
10cm kombu (dried kelp)
30g dried shiitake mushrooms
1 thumb fresh ginger, halved and crushed
4 cloves garlic, smashed
2 tbsp soy sauce
1 tbsp mirin
salt to taste

blanch bones in boiling water, drain and rinse, return to pot with fresh cold water,
bring to boil and reduce heat immediately, char onion and ginger in a dry pan until
blackened, add to pot along with kombu and mushrooms, simmer gently 8-12 hours or
overnight, strain through fine mesh, season with soy sauce and mirin, taste and adjust
salt, cool and store in refrigerator for up to 5 days or freeze
