A [[recipe]] for belgian waffles


300g all-purpose flour
1 tbsp sugar
2 tsp baking powder
1/2 tsp salt
2 eggs, separated
300ml milk
4 tbsp melted butter
1 tsp vanilla extract

preheat waffle iron, whisk flour sugar baking powder and salt, in another bowl
whisk yolks with milk melted butter and vanilla, stir wet into dry just until
combined, beat egg whites to stiff peaks and gently fold into batter, lightly
butter hot waffle iron, pour 3/4 cup batter per waffle, close and cook until
golden and crispy according to iron instructions usually 3-4 minutes, serve
immediately with whipped cream berries and maple syrup
