Ideas for wrapping gifts without buying new paper.

**From around the apartment:**
- Old newspapers, especially the weekend supplements with colour
- Comic strips (feels playful, especially for kids)
- Brown kraft paper from delivery boxes, tie with twine
- Fabric scraps or old tea towels (becomes a gift in itself if nice)
- Maps or old atlases (works well for travel-themed gifts)
- Sheet music or printed lyrics (for musicians)
- Pages torn from old books nobody reads anymore

**Techniques that elevate scrappy materials:**
- Tie bundles with natural twine, raffia, or ribbon I already own
- Add a dried flower, sprig of rosemary, or cinnamon stick on top
- Stamps or drawings in marker on brown paper
- Layer different textures (newspaper + fabric + twine)
- Use washi tape for edges (I have a stash, reusable)

**Things I'm collecting for future wrapping:**
- Interesting junk mail for collage texture
- Dried flowers and herbs from the kitchen
- Ribbon from old gift boxes, unravelled carefully
- Scraps of fancy fabric from project bags

Never buying commercial wrapping paper, the environmental guilt isn't worth it, and honestly the handmade-looking presentation is often better.
