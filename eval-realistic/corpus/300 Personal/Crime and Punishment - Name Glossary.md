Cast list: Raskolnikov, Sonya, Porfiry, Razumikhin, and the rest.

## Central Characters

**Raskolnikov** — Rodion Romanovich, the protagonist. An impoverished, brilliant student who murders an elderly pawnbroker to test his theory that superior individuals transcend conventional morality. Isolated, feverish, consumed by guilt.

**Sonya** — Sofya Semyonovna, a young woman forced into prostitution to support her destitute family. Kind-hearted and devoutly Christian; she becomes Raskolnikov's spiritual redeemer and the moral center of the novel.

**Porfiry Petrovich** — The detective investigating the murders. Shrewd, seemingly bumbling on the surface but perceptive and morally grounded; he gradually closes in on Raskolnikov while attempting to guide him toward confession.

**Razumikhin** — Raskolnikov's friend and former roommate. Practical, loyal, and grounded in normal life; falls in love with Raskolnikov's sister Dunya and represents a healthy counterpoint to Raskolnikov's nihilism.

## Secondary Characters

**Katerina Ivanovna** — Sonya's stepmother, desperate and ill, driven mad by poverty and struggling to maintain dignity while her family crumbles.

**Pulcheria Alexandrovna** — Raskolnikov's mother, loving and faithful, who travels to St. Petersburg to support her son.

**Avdotya Romanovna (Dunya)** — Raskolnikov's sister, intelligent and principled; she nearly becomes engaged to the exploitative businessman Luzhin before being rescued by Razumikhin.

**Arkady Ivanovich Svidrigailov** — A wealthy, libertine nobleman with a mysterious past; represents hedonism and moral corruption without conscience.
