# Recipe Box Organization

A hybrid system for storing recipes—some in a physical card box, others captured in this vault, with rules for deciding which goes where.

## Vault Recipes
Recipes I've tried multiple times and want to iterate on live here in folders by category (baking, sauces, weeknight dinners). I add notes about timing or substitutions as I refine them. The vault makes searching and version control easy when I discover a better technique or want to compare variations.

## Physical Card Box
The box holds printed recipes from magazines and cookbooks, handwritten family recipes, and clippings from older sources. Cards that I use rarely or just want to keep for nostalgia stay here. Standby comfort foods and holiday recipes live in the box so I can cook without reaching for a screen. Every six months I review both and move successful vault recipes to cards if I find myself printing them repeatedly.
