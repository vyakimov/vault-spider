# Running Shoe Rotation

Rotating two pairs of running shoes and when to replace them.

## System
I alternate between two pairs of running shoes to extend their lifespan. Each pair gets ~3-4 runs per week, with rest days between. The midsole material benefits from drying time between runs, and alternating prevents uneven wear on one pair.

## Replacement Cycle
After 400-500 miles per pair (roughly 2 years at my mileage), I retire a pair and buy a new one. I notice decreased responsiveness around 400 miles and increased injury risk if I push further. Tracking mileage helps me know when a replacement is due.
