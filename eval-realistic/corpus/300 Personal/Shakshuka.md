A [[recipe]] for shakshuka


2 tbsp olive oil
1 onion, diced
4 cloves garlic, minced
1 tsp ground cumin
1 tsp paprika
1/4 tsp cayenne
1 can (800g) crushed tomatoes
1/2 cup tomato sauce
salt and pepper to taste
6 eggs
1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
crusty bread for serving

heat oil, sauté onion and garlic until soft, add spices and cook 30 seconds,
add tomatoes and sauce, simmer 10 minutes until sauce thickens, make 6 small
wells in sauce and crack an egg into each, cover and cook 5-8 minutes until
egg whites set but yolks still soft, garnish with cilantro and serve with bread
