Tasting notes on coffee beans tried, separate from the brew method references.

**Ethopian Yirgacheffe** (January, local roaster) — floral, almost wine-like, delicate, tasted different as it cooled
**Colombian single-origin** (February, friend's recommendation) — chocolate and caramel, predictable but solid, good espresso base
**Brazilian Natural process** (March, was on sale) — berry hints, heavier body, good for milk drinks
**Kenyan AA** (March, splurge purchase) — complex, berry and black pepper, best as filter not espresso
**House blend** (currently using) — consistent, no surprises, good for everyday
**Tanzanian Peaberry** (tried once, too acidic) — not for me, gave remainder to Casey

**Notes:** I'm learning that lighter roasts let origin flavours show through, medium roasts are forgiving, dark roasts taste more of the roasting than the origin. Single-origin is more interesting than blends but varies batch to batch. Whole beans vs pre-ground makes a huge difference. Fresh beans (roasted within 2 weeks) taste noticeably better than older stock.
