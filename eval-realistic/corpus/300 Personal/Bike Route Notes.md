# Bike Route Notes

A record of commute routes tested over the past year, including which one to favor on rainy days.

## Dry Weather Routes
The main route along Riverside is scenic but adds 15 minutes. The direct route through downtown is faster but congested during rush hour. I typically alternate between them depending on energy level and time. The scenic route has a long descent into the park that makes the return pedal feel worth it.

## Rainy Weather Route
When wet, I take Westwood Avenue which is flatter and has better drainage despite being less direct. The potholes on Riverside fill with water making it treacherous. Westwood has wider shoulders and fewer cars running red lights in poor visibility. On heavy rain days, I leave 10 minutes earlier to account for slower traction.
