Which plants get watered on which days.

**Monday & Thursday:**
- Snake plant (bedroom, can skip a week if soil is still moist)
- Pothos (living room shelf, roots in water, change every other watering)
- Succulents (windowsill, light spray only)

**Wednesday & Saturday:**
- Monstera (large pot, living room, drains fast in summer)
- Peace lily (bathroom, tell me when it's thirsty by drooping)
- Fiddle leaf fig (near window, needs consistent moisture spring–summer)

**Daily check:** herbs on the kitchen windowsill, they dry out quickly

**Notes:** winter watering is half as often, all pots have drainage holes, I use tap water but let it sit overnight first, the bathroom gets humidity from the shower so the peace lily needs less water than it looks
