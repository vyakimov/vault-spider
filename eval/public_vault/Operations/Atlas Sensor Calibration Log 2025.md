# Atlas Sensor Calibration Log 2025

## Routine Maintenance and Drift Monitoring

In 2025, the five operational Atlas stations transitioned to a bi-annual calibration schedule following two consecutive years of zero drift incidents. This reduces site visit overhead while maintaining the same confidence interval in measurement quality.

## Spring 2025 Campaign (February–April)

- Station AT-01: Humidity +1.1%, temperature −0.1°C (nominal)
- Station AT-02: Both sensors nominal
- Station AT-03: Humidity drift detected at +2.9%; unit recalibrated and logged for replacement consideration in budget cycle
- Station AT-04: Both sensors nominal
- Station AT-05 (new, Sunset District): Factory calibration verified on-site, baseline established

## Fall 2025 Campaign (September–November)

Scheduled for Q4 2025. Technician assignments and site access permits are coordinated through the on-call rotation documented in [[Atlas On-call Rotation]].

## Equipment and Standards

Calibration reference: Fluke 1503A temperature reference (NIST-traceable, next verification due March 2026).
Humidity reference: Vaisala HMP15 probe, calibrated November 2024.

All records filed with the central maintenance tracking system. Historical trend analysis informs decisions about sensor replacement schedules and warranty claims against vendors.
