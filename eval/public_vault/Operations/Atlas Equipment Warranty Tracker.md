## Environmental Sensor Suites

Pressure, temperature, and humidity sensor packages are warrantied for 2 years from installation date. Most sensors deployed in 2023-2024 will require renewal decisions by Q1 2026. Replacement units cost $2,800 per site. Warranty renewal is available at 35% of original cost per year for extended coverage.

## Enclosure and Physical Hardware

All steel and composite enclosures carry 5-year corrosion warranties covering salt-spray environments. Mounting hardware and cable assemblies are typically not warrantied; consider these consumables. The backup appliance in the central office has a 3-year warranty expiring 2027-05-06.

## Related Monitoring

Dashboard alert rules configured in [[Atlas Monitoring Alert Rules]] will trigger 60 days before any warranty expiration. Operators should review expiring warranties monthly and coordinate replacement orders with procurement. Extensions must be purchased before expiry; post-expiry warranty costs increase by 40-50%.
