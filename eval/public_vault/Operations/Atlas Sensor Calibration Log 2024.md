# Atlas Sensor Calibration Log 2024

## Quarterly Validation Schedule

All Atlas humidity and temperature sensor units received a factory-traceable calibration check before deployment. Recalibration events in 2024 were scheduled post-deployment as part of the routine maintenance cycle, with each Cedar gateway station visited once per quarter.

## January–March 2024

- Station AT-01 (Kearny site): Humidity offset +0.8%, temperature within spec
- Station AT-02 (Mission District): Temperature offset −0.3°C, humidity nominal
- Station AT-03 (North Beach): Both sensors recalibrated; original humidity sensor replaced due to drift exceeding tolerance band

## April–June 2024

- Stations AT-01, AT-02: No action required
- Station AT-04 (new deployment, Civic Center): Initial calibration verified against reference standard

## July–December 2024

Routine checks performed on all four operational stations. No units required recalibration during this period; all readings remained within ±2% relative humidity and ±1°C tolerances established in the Atlas design specifications.

Complete calibration certificates are archived in the shared storage under the year-by-site folder structure. Reference data from the calibration equipment (Extech 445815 multifunction meter, serial 48-CCB-2941) is retained for three years per ISO 17025 audit requirements.
