## Ordering and Replenishment

When any stock item drops below 1 unit (except batteries and gaskets, which reorder at 1), the field supervisor notifies the procurement manager to order replacement stock. Lead times vary (see [[Atlas Vendor Contact List]] for typical vendor lead times).

Critical components (sensor modules, modems) are ordered quarterly even if current stock is adequate, given the 3–4 week lead time from international suppliers.

## Calibration and Tracking

All sensor modules in stock are periodically checked against a calibration reference to detect drift. Batteries are load-tested annually. This inventory is distinct from the full warranty exchange program managed by each vendor.
