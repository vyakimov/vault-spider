# Atlas Cost Review 2024

## Annual Operating Expenses Summary

Atlas operating costs for the 2024 calendar year totaled $487,200 across 12 field sites and central infrastructure. This represents a 12.3% increase from 2023 ($433,600), driven primarily by expanded cellular connectivity at three new remote sites and increased cloud storage for backup retention.

## Expense Breakdown by Category

- **Hardware and Equipment**: $128,400 (26%) — Cedar gateway maintenance contracts, sensor calibration kits, replacement enclosures. One-time cost of $34,000 for new pressure sensors at four sites.
- **Cloud and Storage Services**: $156,800 (32%) — PostgreSQL database hosting ($89,200), backup storage ($47,600), dashboard infrastructure ($20,000).
- **Connectivity**: $98,600 (20%) — Wired internet at 10 sites, cellular fallover modems at 2 sites. Average $8,200 per site annually.
- **Personnel**: $89,400 (18%) — Field technician labor (6 FTE at average $14,900 per person-year), on-call rotation stipends.
- **Other**: $14,000 (3%) — Software licenses, training, documentation tools.

## Year-over-Year Growth Analysis

Hardware costs increased 18% due to expanded site count (11 to 12 sites) and sensor replacements. Cloud costs rose 22%, primarily from backup storage growth (2.1TB additional archived data). Personnel increased 8% from addition of one field technician. Connectivity showed modest 3% increase despite two new sites, due to negotiated rate reductions with carriers.

## Cost Projections for 2025

Based on planned expansion to 14 sites and proposed new equipment (cellular modems at 3 additional sites), 2025 costs are projected at $534,000 to $562,000. This assumes no major hardware failures requiring emergency replacements and stable cloud service pricing.

## Recommendations

Consolidation of backup retention policies could reduce storage costs by approximately $6,800 annually. Renegotiation of hardware maintenance contracts for existing Cedar gateways may yield 5-8% savings ($6,400-$10,200). These measures are scheduled for inclusion in the 2025 cost review.
