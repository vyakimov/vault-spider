# Spice Blend - House Curry

## Toasting and Grinding

Toast whole spices in a dry skillet over medium heat for 1–2 minutes: 3 tablespoons coriander seed, 2 tablespoons cumin seed, 1 tablespoon fenugreek, 1 teaspoon black peppercorns, 4–5 dried red chilies, and 2 star anise. Swirl frequently until fragrant but not smoking. Cool on a plate, then grind in a spice mill or mortar and pestle to a fine powder. This base yields about 1 cup of blend.

## Blending Final Spices

After grinding, fold in 2 tablespoons turmeric powder, 1 tablespoon ginger powder, 1 teaspoon garlic powder, and a pinch of asafoetida (hing). Store in an airtight jar away from light and heat. The blend stays potent for 3–4 months and develops deeper notes after a week of settling.

## Using the Blend

For a pot of curry (4–6 servings), temper 2–3 tablespoons of the house blend in oil or ghee at medium heat for 30–45 seconds before adding vegetables or protein. This blooming step releases the aromatic oils. Add vegetables and stock, then simmer gently. A pinch of sugar or a squeeze of lime balances the warmth without masking the spice profile.

## Storing Whole Spices

If batch-toasting feels tedious, buy whole spices in smaller quantities and toast-and-grind weekly. Store whole spices in airtight containers in a cool, dark cabinet (not near the stove heat). For more details on balance and technique, see [[Jam Setting Point Notes]].
