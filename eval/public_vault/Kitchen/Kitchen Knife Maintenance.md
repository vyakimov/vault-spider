# Kitchen Knife Maintenance

## Weekly Honing

Use a steel or ceramic honing rod every week before or after heavy prep. Draw the knife spine-first across the rod at a 15–20° angle, alternating sides 4–5 times per side. Honing realigns the knife's microscopic edge but does not remove metal. A quick hone takes 30 seconds and keeps the knife sharp between deeper sharpening sessions.

## Sharpening Schedule

Professional sharpening or whetstone work every 3–4 months, depending on kitchen use. A dull knife requires more force and is a safety risk; it's better to sharpen proactively. If you sharpen at home, use a two-stone progression: 1000–2000 grit for initial edge correction, then 6000–8000 grit for refinement. A 17–20° angle works for most kitchen knives; Asian knives often prefer 15–17°.

## Immediate Care

Wash and dry knives by hand immediately after use. Never put sharp knives in the dishwasher, and do not leave them soaking in water. Store in a knife block, magnetic strip, or sleeve to prevent dulling and safety hazards. Keep the kitchen block away from moisture to prevent rust.

## Maintaining Team Use

Post a simple sign on the knife block: "Hone before use, wash immediately, store dry." When shared knives get dull, everyone in the household should be comfortable honing them quickly.
