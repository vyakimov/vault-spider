# Sourdough Starter Log

## Weekly Feeding Schedule

Feed the starter Tuesday and Saturday mornings at room temperature (68–72°F). Use a 1:1:1 ratio (starter:water:flour by weight). A 50g starter + 50g filtered water + 50g whole wheat or unbleached all-purpose gives reliable rises within 4–6 hours. If the kitchen runs cold (below 65°F), add an extra feed Wednesday to maintain vigor.

## Rise Observations

After feeding, the starter should double within 5–7 hours under normal kitchen conditions. A thick, glossy dome indicates proper fermentation; if you see pink streaks or a nail-polish smell, discard and rebuild. The smell should be pleasant yeast and slight tartness by hour 6. Gray liquid on top (hooch) means the starter is hungry—stir it back in or proceed with feeding.

## Cold-Storage Protocol

Before long absences, reduce feeding to once per week and cover loosely with a towel in the coldest corner of the fridge (40–42°F). The starter can hold this way for up to 3 weeks. Revive by returning to room temperature and feeding daily for 3 days.

## Troubleshooting

Thin, runny starter after feeding suggests too much water or very warm kitchen; reduce water proportion or use cooler water. No activity after 8 hours at room temp indicates weak starter or very cold room—try moving to a warmer spot (top of the fridge works) or warming a bowl of water underneath.
