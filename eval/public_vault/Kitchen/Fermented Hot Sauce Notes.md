# Fermented Hot Sauce Notes

## Basic Brine Ratio

Mix 5% salt by weight of the peppers and water combined. For 500g fresh peppers + 250g filtered water, dissolve 37.5g sea salt in the water first, then pack chopped peppers (stems removed, seeds kept for heat) into a clean quart jar, pour brine to cover, and weight with a small glass to keep peppers submerged. A piece of cabbage or cloth under the weight prevents surface mold.

## Fermentation Timeline

Ferment at room temperature (65–72°F) for 2–4 weeks. Taste from day 7 onward; the sauce develops tanginess and the red peppers deepen in flavor by week 2. Small white or tan surface yeast is normal and protective; skim off any pink or black mold immediately. The brine should smell pleasantly sour and peppery, never rotten.

## Finishing Steps

After fermentation, blend the peppers with 2–3 tablespoons of the remaining brine until smooth. Strain through cheesecloth if a thinner sauce is preferred. The finished sauce keeps refrigerated for 6 months; for shelf stability, pack into sterilized jars and heat-process if you have canning experience.
