# Granola Recipe

## Base Mix

Combine 3 cups old-fashioned oats, 1 cup raw almonds (or mixed nuts), 1/2 cup unsweetened coconut flakes, and 1/4 cup pumpkin seeds in a large bowl. In a small saucepan, warm 1/3 cup coconut oil with 3 tablespoons maple syrup and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract. Pour the wet mixture over the dry and stir until every piece is coated. Spread thinly on parchment-lined baking sheets.

## Baking and Cooling

Bake at 300°F for 20–25 minutes, stirring every 5–7 minutes to promote even browning and prevent burning. The granola should turn golden and smell nutty; remove it slightly before you think it's done (carryover heat continues cooking). Cool completely on the baking sheets—it will crisp up as it cools. Trying to break up warm granola makes it crumble; wait 30 minutes for maximum crunch.

## Storage and Serving

Once cool, stir in 1/2 cup unsweetened dried fruit (raisins, cranberries, or apricots) and store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 weeks. The granola keeps longer if you store the dried fruit separately and mix it in just before serving. Serve over yogurt or with milk as a breakfast cereal.
