# Kitchen Equipment Inventory

## Appliances and Status

- Oven: Whirlpool convection, model WEC310S. Thermostat calibrated February 2024; runs 25–30°F hot. Compensate by reducing dial by 25°F or baking lower in the oven.
- Refrigerator: Stainless Maytag French-door, 25 cu ft. Works well; fridge side runs 37–40°F, freezer runs −2°F. Do not block the back vents.
- Stovetop: Six-burner gas, electric ignition occasionally sticks on the front-left burner. Light manually with a lighter if needed.
- Dishwasher: Bosch, quiet model, cycle time 2.5 hours on heavy wash. Uses spot-free rinse; avoid overloading.

## Prep Equipment

- Cutting boards: Three—one plastic (meat), two wood (vegetables, bread). Oil wood boards monthly.
- Knife block and honing steel: Carbon steel knives (8-inch chef, paring, serrated). Hone weekly; professional sharpen quarterly.
- Food processor: Cuisinart 14-cup, with S-blade and grater attachment. Motor runs loud but is reliable.
- Mixer: KitchenAid stand mixer, stainless bowl, multiple attachments (dough hook, flat beater, whip).

## Storage and Small Appliances

- Food scale: Baking precision scale, 5kg max. Batteries died March 2026; new batteries stored in the drawer.
- Thermometers: Two probe thermometers and one instant-read. Instant-read reads 1–2°F high.
- Mason jars: Assorted sizes, 36 total, stored in the lower cabinet. Lids are good for a second season; replace annually.
