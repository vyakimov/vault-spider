# Community Plot Assignment Rules

## Annual Assignment Process

In February of each year, all current plot holders must confirm their intention to retain a plot for the upcoming season. Forms are submitted by 28 February; failure to submit results in plot reassignment. New applicants are added to a waiting list and assigned plots in March based on seniority (returning gardeners first), geographic preference, and bed size preference.

Each volunteer receives one 1.2 m × 2.4 m raised bed. Gardeners managing multiple family members may petition for a second bed; approval is granted only if waiting-list demand does not exceed available inventory.

## Maintenance Standards and Abandonment

Plots must be actively cultivated throughout the growing season (May–September). "Actively cultivated" means weeding, watering, and harvesting at least twice weekly. Three consecutive weeks of neglect (assessed by visual inspection on Sundays) triggers a warning notice; four weeks of neglect results in forfeiture.

An unmaintained plot is reclaimed and reassigned within one week. Remaining plants and personal tools are removed by staff and stored for 14 days; unclaimed items are donated or composted.

## Succession and Resources

See [[Native Plant List]] for approved perennial plantings in community beds. Volunteers follow [[Companion Planting Notes]] guidelines for annual plantings to ensure crop compatibility and reduce pest pressure across the garden. Detailed guidance on plot management, tool access, and resource allocation appears in the community handbook available from the garden coordinator.
