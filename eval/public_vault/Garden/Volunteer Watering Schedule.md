# Volunteer Watering Schedule

## Rotation and Duty Weeks

From 15 June through 31 August, the community garden operates on a rotating volunteer watering schedule. Six volunteers are assigned one-week duties in sequence; each volunteer is responsible for providing supplemental irrigation during their assigned week.

The rotation is:
- Week 1–2 (15–28 June): Volunteer A
- Week 3–4 (29 June–12 July): Volunteer B
- Week 5–6 (13–26 July): Volunteer C
- Week 7–8 (27 July–9 August): Volunteer D
- Week 9–10 (10–23 August): Volunteer E
- Week 11–12 (24 August–31 August): Volunteer F

Each volunteer covers Friday through Thursday watering (5 days per week). Duty includes early-morning watering (before 08:00) to minimize evaporative loss.

## Watering Protocol

Deep soaking twice weekly is preferred to daily light sprinkling. Focus on newly planted seedlings and drought-sensitive crops (squash, cucumber). Established perennials (herbs, fruit trees) receive supplemental water only during extreme heat (above 28°C).

Rainwater barrels provide priority water during dry spells. Hand-watering with the garden hose supplements barrel supplies. Approximately 200 liters per watering session are typical for full community coverage.

## Contingency and Flexibility

If a volunteer becomes unavailable mid-week, they arrange a swap with the following week's volunteer. The garden coordinator maintains a backup volunteer list for emergency coverage. During periods of sustained rain (25+ mm per week), the watering schedule is suspended to prevent overwatering.
