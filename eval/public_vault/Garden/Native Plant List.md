# Native Plant List

## Recommended Species for Pollinator Border

All plants listed below are native to the temperate region and are recommended for the garden's pollinator strip and native plantings:

**Early Spring Bloomers (April–May)**
- Creeping Phlox (Phlox subulata) — low-growing, prolific flower production
- Helleborus niger (Christmas Rose) — evergreen, attracts early bees
- Columbine (Aquilegia canadensis) — red tubular flowers for hummingbirds

**Mid-Season Bloomers (June–August)**
- Black-eyed Susan (Rudbeckia fulgida) — prolific, drought-tolerant, self-seeding
- Purple Coneflower (Echinacea purpurea) — tall stems, excellent for mixed borders
- Salvia guaranitica (Anise Sage) — bright blue flowers, attracts hummingbirds and native bees
- Blazing Star (Liatris spicata) — purple flower spikes, excellent for butterflies

**Late Season Bloomers (September–October)**
- New England Aster (Symphyotrichum novae-angliae) — late nectar source for migrating monarchs
- Goldenrod (Solidago virgaurea subsp. leiocarpa) — does not cause allergies, excellent for late-season foraging

## Spacing and Installation Notes

Most plantings are installed in spring (April) or fall (August–September). Space plants at mature width (typically 40–50 cm apart) to allow room for growth without overcrowding. All plants perform best with minimal supplemental watering once established (after season one).
