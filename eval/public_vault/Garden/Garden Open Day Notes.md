# Garden Open Day Notes

## Annual Event Planning

The community garden hosts an open day each June, typically on the second Saturday. The event runs from 10:00 to 16:00 and is open to the public, potential volunteers, and neighboring community members. Approximately 80–120 visitors attend annually.

Planning begins in April; a volunteer coordinator assumes lead responsibility for scheduling volunteers, arranging refreshments, and publicizing the event through neighborhood channels and local media.

## Station Setup and Demonstrations

**Seedling Propagation Station**: Volunteer demonstrates [[Cold Frame Construction]] techniques, showing seedling trays in a working cold frame and discussing hardening-off protocols. Visitors are encouraged to pot a seedling to take home.

**Tool Care and Sharpening**: Using the community's tool collection, volunteer demonstrates proper sharpening techniques and allows visitors to examine various hand tools up close.

**Compost and Soil Preparation**: A decomposition display shows material at different stages (fresh scraps, partially decomposed, finished compost). Volunteer invites questions about home composting and the principles of decomposition.

**Trellis and Vertical Growing**: Active [[Trellis Building Notes]] examples (bean vines, cucumber trellises) are displayed with photos showing construction and seasonal progress.

**Plant ID and Pollinator Support**: Volunteer staffs a table discussing garden biodiversity and the importance of supporting pollinators, referencing the [[Pollinator Strip Plan]] and its role in the community garden.

## Logistics

The [[Garden Shed Inventory]] is reviewed in advance to ensure tools for demonstration are in good condition. [[Volunteer Watering Schedule]] coordinators confirm that irrigation systems are functioning before the event. Refreshments (lemonade, cookies) are provided at a donation table. Signage directing visitors to station locations is posted at the garden entrance and throughout the beds.

Attendance is recorded; contact information is collected for future volunteer recruitment.

## Seasonal Coordination

The open day occurs during the peak growing season (June), coordinating with [[Seed Starting Calendar]] activities. Volunteers often use the open day as an opportunity to share seedlings and plant divisions with interested community members.
