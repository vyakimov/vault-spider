# Mercury Observation Notes - March 2025

## Observation Session - March 8, 2025

Clear sky conditions with atmospheric transparency rated 7/10 (moderate seeing). Mercury was visible 20 minutes after sunset at 18.5 degrees elevation. Captured 847 frames at 60fps using [[Mercury Observation Equipment List|Equipment List]]. Estimated angular diameter 8.2 arcseconds; phase illumination approximately 42% crescent.

## Atmospheric Conditions and Challenges

Temperature inversion layer at 2km altitude created shimmer effects, reducing image sharpness in the 300-500nm wavelength bands. Wind gusts up to 15 knots destabilized the telescope during peak observing window (18:35-19:15 UTC). Humidity at 68% with dew point approaching telescope optics by end of session.

## Data Quality Assessment

Of 847 captured frames, 612 met quality threshold (>80% MTF in central pixel region). Seeing deterioration around 19:00 UTC reduced usable data rate from 15% to 3% of captured frames. Post-processing with wavelet deconvolution recovered detail in 89 additional marginal frames.

## Noteworthy Observations

Three small limb-darkening features consistent with atmospheric dynamics. Upper cloud formations show motion consistent with 25 m/s wind velocities. Recorded spectral data shows absorption signature at 589nm possibly related to sodium emission from solar wind interaction.
