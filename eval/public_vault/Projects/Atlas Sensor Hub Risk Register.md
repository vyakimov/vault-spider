# Atlas Sensor Hub Risk Register

## Supply Chain Risks

Gateway hardware lead time of 14-18 weeks creates schedule risk if vendors encounter manufacturing constraints. Mitigated by placing initial order 6 months in advance and establishing secondary vendor relationship. Sensor module availability from two sources reduces single-vendor dependency.

## Technical Risks

Firmware stability under extreme temperature swings remains unvalidated at deployment sites above 2000m elevation. Mitigation: run extended pre-deployment testing at altitude before Phase 2 expansion to high-elevation sites. Concurrent development of rollback procedures to safely recover from field firmware issues.

## Operational Risks

Field technician training completion is on critical path; any training delay cascades to installation timeline. Addressed through pre-training cohort of 12 technicians and documented procedures enabling supervised-yet-independent operations by month three.

## External Dependencies

Regional network operator SLA commitments are required for backhaul reliability. [[Mercury Observation Notes - March 2025]] provides context on external observational constraints that may impact deployment site accessibility.
