# Atlas Sensor Vendor Evaluation - Battery Packs

## Candidates Under Review

Three vendors provided battery pack modules compatible with Atlas sensor enclosures:

- **ThermoLink BPX-48**: Lithium-iron phosphate, 48Wh nominal, temperature range -10 to 50°C, weight 1.2 kg, 500 cycle rating
- **Sunbright SB-LiFePO**: LiFePO4 chemistry, 60Wh, -20 to 55°C range, weight 1.4 kg, 1000 cycle rating
- **Kronos PowerCell Pro**: Hybrid lead-carbon, 40Ah capacity at 12V nominal, weight 3.1 kg, 2000 cycle rating

## Performance Criteria

Each pack was tested for:
- Capacity retention after 50 charge cycles at 80% depth of discharge
- Cold-weather discharge performance (8 hours at -5°C under 5A load)
- Physical fit within sensor enclosure dimensions (max 12cm height, 8cm width)
- Self-discharge rate over 30-day idle period

## Preliminary Assessment

ThermoLink and Sunbright both meet the temperature and physical constraints. Sunbright's cycle rating and lower self-discharge justify the 18% cost premium. Kronos significantly exceeds weight budget for distributed sensor nodes but may suit gateway backup power. Decision pending final cycle-life testing expected in June 2026.
