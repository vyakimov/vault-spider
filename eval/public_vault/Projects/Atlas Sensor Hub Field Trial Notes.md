# Atlas Sensor Hub Field Trial Notes

## Initial Observations

Two trial stations deployed in mid-September 2024 at elevation and urban canyon locations. Both stations achieved data collection within first 24 hours. Environmental conditions varied significantly: the highland site experienced temperatures from -2°C to 18°C over the trial period, while the urban site remained 8-12°C warmer throughout.

## Sensor Performance

Humidity sensors occasionally drifted in the urban canyon location, likely due to building air management systems creating local microclimates. Repositioning sensor intake to the external wall face resolved the issue. Temperature readings showed excellent correlation with independent validation instruments (within 0.3°C at both sites).

## Field Technician Feedback

Installation time improved significantly at the second site after incorporating lessons from the first. Access issues encountered at both locations required coordination with building occupants; a site pre-authorization checklist was added to prevent future delays. Equipment mounting proved more fragile than expected in high-wind conditions; bracket redesign completed before Phase 1.
