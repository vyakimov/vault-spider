# Atlas Sensor Hub Cost Estimate

## Hardware and Installation

Per-station hardware cost ranges from $3,200 to $4,100 depending on sensor complement. Installation labor averages 8 hours per site at regional technician rates. Initial deployment of 20 stations (Phase 1 and 2) totals approximately $78,000 in hardware plus $32,000 in field labor.

## Infrastructure and Operations

Annual operational costs projected at $12,000 for network connectivity, $8,000 for maintenance and component replacement, and $6,000 for firmware updates and platform support. Assumes no major equipment failures requiring gateway replacement.

## Assumptions and Risks

Estimate assumes standard site conditions without major structural work. Sites requiring electrical infrastructure upgrades could add $2,000-$5,000 per location. Long lead times on specialty sensors (currently 16 weeks) may require mid-project procurement adjustments.
