# Mercury Observation Equipment List

## Telescope and Optics

Main instrument: 12-inch Ritchey-Chrétien reflector (f/8 configuration), focal length 2400mm, aperture diameter 305mm. Secondary mirror diameter 75mm, obstruction ratio 5.2%. Tube rings mounted on German equatorial mount with computerized RA/Dec drives for tracking.

## Eyepieces and Magnification

- 8mm eyepiece (300x magnification, 0.13° field of view) — high-resolution lunar and planetary detail
- 20mm eyepiece (120x magnification, 0.33° field of view) — medium magnification for extended viewing
- Barlow lens (3x magnification multiplier) for additional magnification options

## Filters

- Densitometer neutral density filters (ND8, ND16) for bright sky observation during twilight
- Chromatic filters: 589nm (sodium-D line), 656nm (H-alpha), 486nm (H-beta) for spectral analysis
- Polarizing filter for glare reduction during high sun angle observations

## Imaging Equipment

ZWO ASI290MM monochrome camera, 1936x1216 pixel array, 2.4µm pixel size. ROI output at 60fps enables high frame rate capture during peak seeing conditions. Edmund Optics diagonal and 1.25-inch adapter tube for camera interface.

## Supporting Equipment

Motorized focuser with 0.01mm step resolution for precise focus tracking. Dew heater strips around objective cell to prevent condensation during thermal transitions. Red LED headlamp for dark adaptation during session setup.
