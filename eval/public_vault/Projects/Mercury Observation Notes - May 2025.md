# Mercury Observation Notes - May 2025

## Observation Session - May 22, 2025

Scheduled observation during evening elongation with Mercury at maximum western elongation of 18.7 degrees. Visibility window extended to 35 minutes due to favorable latitude. Captured 1200+ frames at lower magnification (60x) compared to March observations to maximize field clarity.

## Lighting and Illumination

Phase illumination measured at 68% through comparative photometry against lunar reference. This represents progression toward superior conjunction; angular separation from sun increased to 22 degrees by session end. Surface albedo estimates from photometric analysis suggest iron oxide abundance consistent with previous spectral studies.

## Equipment Performance

All equipment operated nominally without thermal issues despite ambient temperature of 16°C. Air mass during observation ranged from 2.8 to 1.9 as Mercury climbed in the western sky. Seeing conditions remained stable at 6/10 throughout the session with minimal atmospheric turbulence.

## Data Collection Results

Achieved 85% frame quality rate with post-processing; preliminary analysis shows cloud band structure consistent with atmospheric circulation models at mid-latitudes. Confirmed detection of previously-observed thermal anomaly on sunward hemisphere, supporting hypothesis of enhanced surface heating from solar radiation concentration.
