# Atlas Decision Log - Q2 2025

## Decision: Extend Battery Evaluation Window

**Date:** 2025-04-03 | **Status:** Approved

Delayed battery pack selection from April to June 2025 to gather additional cycle-life data under field conditions. One candidate (Sunbright SB-LiFePO) showed promise in cold-weather testing but required confirmation on long-duration discharge patterns.

## Decision: Cellular Failover as Default Behavior

**Date:** 2025-05-02 | **Status:** Approved

Mandated dual-SIM failover on all Cedar gateways deployed after May 1. Secondary carrier GPRS access now standard; gateways switch to secondary within 3 minutes of primary network detection failure. Reduces human intervention for regional carrier outages.

## Decision: Engage Mercury Project Cross-Learning

**Date:** 2025-05-15 | **Status:** Approved

Established technical liaison with [[Mercury Data Migration]] team to share lessons on large-scale data validation and ingestion pipelines. Atlas Harbor's batch validation logic and Mercury's row-level checksumming can inform each other's design iterations.

## Q2 2025 Vendor Shortlist Update

Finalized [[Atlas Sensor Hub Vendor Shortlist]] for Phase 3 sensor procurement. New suppliers approved for enclosures and LoRaWAN modules; cost reductions of 12–15% negotiated for bulk orders.

## Notes

Q2 2025 marked the transition from initial rollout troubleshooting into scaling operations. Decisions recorded here focus on systems reliability and cost optimization.
