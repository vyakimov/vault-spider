# Atlas Sensor Vendor Evaluation - Enclosures

## Weatherproof Candidates

Evaluated three enclosure manufacturers for sensor node deployment:

- **IsoBox TH-350**: Polycarbonate, 200 x 150 x 100 mm, rated IP66, -30 to +70°C material range, mounting rails for DIN components
- **RuCon StainlessShield**: Stainless steel 304, 250 x 200 x 120 mm, rated IP67, -40 to +80°C, integral humidity drain holes
- **Pelican 1400**: Polymer composite, 425 x 300 x 180 mm, rated IP67, -45 to +60°C, removable pressure relief valve

## Environmental Testing Results

Enclosures were exposed to 200 hours combined thermal cycling (-20 to +60°C), spray salt fog per ASTM B117 for 500 hours, and UV radiation simulating 18 months of outdoor exposure. IsoBox and RuCon maintained IP rating throughout; Pelican developed minor haze on optics post-UV.

## Deployment Decision

IsoBox selected for primary sensor nodes: optimal size for pole mounting, minimal material degradation, and cost-effective replacement. RuCon reserved for corrosive coastal locations. Coordinate enclosure delivery with [[Atlas Sensor Hub Onboarding Guide]] pre-deployment logistics.

## Maintenance Notes

All selected enclosures require annual gasket inspection and semi-annual vent plug cleaning in high-dust environments.
