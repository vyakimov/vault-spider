# Atlas Sensor Hub Rollout - Phase 2

## Geographic Expansion

Phase 2 extends coverage to the Central and South regions, adding twelve stations across six sites. This phase leverages operational knowledge from Phase 1 to accelerate deployment cycles and improve first-time success rates.

## Deployment Methodology Refinements

Incorporate lessons from Phase 1 station setup into updated installation procedures. Pre-position spare components at two regional hubs to reduce mean time to repair. Establish local field technician teams rather than rotating central staff.

## Network Infrastructure Scaling

Coordinate with existing network operators to upgrade backhaul capacity at central aggregation points. Plan for redundant path availability to ensure no single point of failure for data delivery.

## Target Timeline

Phase 2 deployment scheduled to complete within nine months, beginning immediately after Phase 1 stabilization confirmation. Overlapping training of South region field staff to ensure readiness as Phase 1 concludes.
