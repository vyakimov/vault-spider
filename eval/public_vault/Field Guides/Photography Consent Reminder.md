# Photography Consent Reminder

## Policy Statement

All photographs taken in the field for documentation, reporting, or public-facing materials must not contain any identifiable person. This includes faces, distinctive clothing with visible logos, vehicle license plates, or any other detail that could connect a photograph to a specific individual.

The field station is public-safe; we do not collect personal data about visitors or field staff members in any deliverables.

## If Someone Walks Into Your Shot

Pause the photograph or video recording immediately and request that the visitor step out of the frame. Reframe your shot to capture only the equipment, landscape, or other non-identifying details. If the person is unwilling to move, do not proceed with the photograph at that moment.

For candid shots (e.g., someone adjusting sensor equipment), always obtain verbal consent before taking or publishing the image. Retain a note in your field notebook confirming the date and the person's consent.

## Field Data vs. Public Reporting

Field notebooks and technical reports may include initials (e.g., "Visit conducted by V.Y.") for internal accountability. Public reports, web content, and presentations must omit personal identifiers entirely. When describing fieldwork, use generic descriptions: "A team member replaced the rain gauge" rather than naming an individual.
