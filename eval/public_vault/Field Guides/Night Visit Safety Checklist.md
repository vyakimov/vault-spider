# Night Visit Safety Checklist

## Before Departing for Night Work

- [ ] Confirm weather forecast: no storms, wind < 20 km/h, visibility expected to be fair
- [ ] Designate a buddy; two team members minimum for any after-dark station visit
- [ ] Charge both team members' headlamps and confirm beam brightness
- [ ] Pack two spare AA batteries for each headlamp (3 sets total minimum)
- [ ] Bring a handheld emergency beacon or personal locator device
- [ ] Verify vehicle fuel level is at least three-quarters full
- [ ] Check that cell phones are fully charged and service is confirmed in the area
- [ ] File a trip plan with the station office: departure time, expected return, which station, primary contact number

## Lighting and Visibility

- [ ] Wear a high-visibility vest or jacket over fieldwork clothing
- [ ] Keep a powerful flashlight (1000+ lumens) in the vehicle as a backup
- [ ] Affix a red warning light to the vehicle (if parked on or near a road)
- [ ] Do not rely solely on headlamp lighting while walking; use the handheld flashlight to sweep the ground ahead

## Safety Communication

- [ ] Make a radio check-in call to the station office upon arrival at the site
- [ ] Make a second check-in when leaving the site to return to the vehicle
- [ ] Inform the office of any unexpected delays or equipment issues before departing
- [ ] Keep the handheld radio on and accessible throughout the visit

Night visits are limited to equipment checks and battery replacements; do not conduct extensive repairs or investigations after dark.
