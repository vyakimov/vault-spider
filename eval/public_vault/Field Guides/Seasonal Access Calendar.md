# Seasonal Access Calendar

## North Ridge Access

| Month | Access Type | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| January–February | Vehicle (4WD recommended) | Seasonal snow/ice; check county plow schedule |
| March | Vehicle (2WD acceptable) | Road drying out; gate code current for Q1 |
| April–May | Vehicle (2WD acceptable) | Service road may be muddy mid-April; high water downstream affects travel time |
| June–September | Vehicle (standard sedan acceptable) | Optimal season; weekly visits typical |
| October | Vehicle (standard sedan acceptable) | Beginning of fall; visit frequency drops to biweekly |
| November–December | Vehicle (2WD acceptable) | Road conditions variable; Q4 gate code in effect from October 1 |

North Ridge has year-round vehicle access. Visits may be postponed only in extreme weather (blizzards, severe ice) or if the access road is explicitly closed by county emergency management.

## South Meadow Access

| Month | Access Type | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| January–April | Foot-only (biweekly maximum) | Boardwalk may be icy; winter boots and beacons required |
| May | Boardwalk transition | Early May: check creek crossing first; detour may be needed |
| June–October | Vehicle to boardwalk + foot | Primary access route; vehicle reaches parking area; boardwalk is safe |
| November–December | Foot-only (monthly maximum) | Boardwalk icing risk; East Spur closed by padlocked gate |

During the May transition, confirm creek conditions with the station coordinator before scheduling a visit. The boardwalk is inspected annually in late March; if repairs are needed, access may be restricted into mid-April.

## Summary

North Ridge is accessible year-round by vehicle with appropriate precautions. South Meadow transitions to foot-only access from November through April.
