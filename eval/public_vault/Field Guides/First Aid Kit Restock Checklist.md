# First Aid Kit Restock Checklist

## Spring Restock (Before April Field Season)

- [ ] Count and replace all adhesive bandages (aim for 20+ units per kit)
- [ ] Check expiration dates on antibiotic ointment and pain relievers; discard if past date
- [ ] Replace trauma gauze pads (4x4 and 2x2 sizes, minimum 5 of each)
- [ ] Refill blister-treatment supplies and athletic tape
- [ ] Verify tweezers and scissors are sharp and clean; replace if damaged
- [ ] Inspect elastic bandages for elastic loss and brittleness; replace compromised units
- [ ] Check that emergency contact info card is current and laminated

## Fall Restock (Before October Night Visits)

- [ ] Recount bandages and replace any used stock
- [ ] Check all medications for signs of moisture or spoilage
- [ ] Replace antiseptic wipes (moisture can evaporate over months of field use)
- [ ] Ensure the kit is stored in a cool, dry location between visits

## Kit Sign-Off

After each seasonal restock, the person completing the work must sign and date a label attached to the kit. This provides a rapid audit trail if anyone needs to confirm when supplies were last verified.

Kits are stored in two locations: one in the equipment cabinet at North Ridge and one in the vehicle used for station visits. The vehicle kit should be checked monthly on an easy-to-remember date (e.g., the first Monday of each month).
