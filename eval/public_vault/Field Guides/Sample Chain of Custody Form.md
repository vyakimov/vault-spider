# Sample Chain of Custody Form

## Form Purpose

Soil and water samples collected from the field must be tracked through every step from collection to analysis to maintain integrity and traceability. A chain of custody form follows the sample and is signed by every person who handles it.

## Required Fields

Each entry in the chain of custody log should include:

- **Sample ID**: Unique identifier (e.g., SM-2026-07-15-01 for South Meadow collected July 15, 2026)
- **Collection Date and Time**: When the sample was taken (to the nearest 5-minute interval)
- **Collected By**: Name and initials of the person who collected the sample
- **Collection Location**: GPS coordinates or a specific descriptor (e.g., "0.5 m east of solar array")
- **Sample Type**: Soil, water, sediment, or other
- **Depth (if applicable)**: How deep in the soil the sample was taken
- **Container Type**: Vial, tube, jar, or other vessel; record volume if relevant
- **Preservative Used**: None, acid, formalin, or other preservation method
- **Handoff Date and Time**: When the sample was transferred to the next handler
- **Received By**: Printed name and initials of the person receiving the sample
- **Storage Condition**: Refrigerated, frozen, ambient, etc.
- **Destination Lab**: Name of the laboratory that will analyze the sample

## Handling Continuity

Do not leave a sample unattended. If the sample must be stored temporarily (e.g., overnight before transport to the lab), note the storage location and the date/time of storage on the form.

Never transport samples in a personal vehicle; use only the designated field vehicle, and keep the form with the sample container at all times.
