# Trailhead Access Notes

## North Ridge Trailhead

The North Ridge station is accessible year-round via the main paved access road. A locked gate (code rotates quarterly; current code valid through June 30) opens onto a 0.8 km dirt track. Four-wheel-drive is unnecessary except during the worst mud conditions in April.

Two parking pullouts provide space for up to three vehicles. Do not block the gate or the internal service road.

## South Meadow: Seasonal Route Changes

The primary boardwalk route to South Meadow remains open May through October. During late May and early June, the creek crossing (see Water Crossing notes) may require the detour via East Spur.

The East Spur road is impassable November through March; a gate is padlocked at the top of the ridgeline during this period. Notify the station coordinator if you encounter the gate open outside season.

## Gate Code Rotation Schedule

- Q1 (January–March): 4827
- Q2 (April–June): 6149
- Q3 (July–September): 3592
- Q4 (October–December): 8034

Codes are changed on the first workday of each quarter. Do not share codes via email or texts; they are given verbally at the monthly field crew meetings only.
