# Emergency Contact Card Template

## Card Format

Print laminated wallet-sized cards (3" × 5" minimum) for all field team members. Each card fits in a shirt pocket or equipment pack. Cards should be waterproof and include the following information:

---

**EMERGENCY CONTACTS - Field Station**

**NEAREST HOSPITAL:**
Mountain View Regional Medical Center
1045 Ridge Road, Mountain View, CO 80401
Emergency: 911
Non-emergency line: (303) 555-0147
Distance: 18 km, 20-minute drive from North Ridge

**STATION COORDINATOR:**
Maria Santos
Office: (303) 555-0188
Cell: (720) 555-4201
Email: m.santos@fieldstation.edu

**FIELD STATION OFFICE (24-hour voicemail):**
(303) 555-0155

---

## Card Distribution

Each team member receives two cards at the start of the field season: one for personal use and one to carry in the vehicle first-aid kit. Replacement cards are available from the station coordinator.

Review and update the card annually in December to confirm all phone numbers remain current and the nearest hospital location is still accurate.
