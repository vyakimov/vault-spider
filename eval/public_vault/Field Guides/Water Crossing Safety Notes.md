# Water Crossing Safety Notes

## Creek Crossing Seasonal Timing

The creek crossing to South Meadow is normally safe to ford on foot from late May through mid-October. The crossing is shallow (typically 0.5 m) and is rocky enough to provide good footing.

From mid-October through May, water levels rise due to spring snowmelt and winter rains. During this period, ford depth can exceed 1.0 metre and current can be swift. The crossing becomes impassable when water velocity looks fast enough to move a stick downstream in less than 2 seconds.

## Assessment Before Crossing

Before wading into any creek, observe flow for at least one minute:

- If debris (branches, leaves) is moving rapidly, the current is unsafe; do not attempt to cross
- If water appears discoloured or turbid, the creek has been fed by recent heavy rain and may rise unexpectedly
- If the water temperature is noticeably cold (below 10°C by hand test), treat the crossing as unsafe and use the detour route

Never ford alone; always have a buddy observing from higher ground.

## Detour Route

When the creek is unsafe, use the East Spur detour road. This route adds 45 minutes to travel time but avoids water contact. The detour is open May through October only; the padlocked gate closes November through April.

In late April and early May, check the gate lock status; if the gate has been unlocked by the maintenance crew, the detour may be passable before the official opening date.
