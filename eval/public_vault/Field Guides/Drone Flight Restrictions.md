# Drone Flight Restrictions

## Altitude Ceiling

Unmanned aerial vehicle (drone) operations above the field station are restricted to a maximum altitude of 50 metres above ground level (AGL). This limit protects low-level aircraft traffic and maintains visual line-of-sight control.

Flights above 50 m AGL, or flights planned to exceed 50 m for more than a few seconds, require FAA Part 107 certification and must be filed with the county airspace authority at least 48 hours in advance. The station coordinator can assist with filing procedures.

## No-Fly Zone Around Research Building

A 100-metre buffer zone is established around the research building (1 km south of North Ridge station). No drone flights are permitted within this perimeter, regardless of altitude, due to administrative radio antennae and sensitive office equipment in the building.

The boundary is marked on the station site map with a dashed line.

## Pre-Flight Notification

Anyone planning a drone survey flight must notify the station coordinator and all field team members at least one week in advance. A brief email listing the date, time, location, and mission objective is sufficient.

This notification allows staff to clear the area if they plan to be on-site and alerts the coordinator to monitor for unexpected aircraft in the region.
