# Wildlife Encounter Protocol

## Distance and Retreat

If any large ungulate (deer, elk) is sighted:

- Maintain at least 30 metres distance at all times
- Make noise (voice or whistle) and move perpendicular to the animal's path to avoid startling it
- Do not run; move slowly and deliberately back toward the vehicle or station

If an animal shows interest by moving toward you or stopping to watch, increase the distance to 50 metres and alert the station coordinator by radio.

## Sighting Log Fields

Record all encounters in the field notebook. Required fields:

- Date and time to the nearest 5-minute interval
- Species and estimated count (e.g., "2 mule deer does")
- Location and GPS coordinates if recording position is available
- Behavior (feeding, alert, fleeing)
- Weather and wind direction at time of sighting
- Notes on any unusual observations (limping, vocalizations, fresh tracks nearby)

## Rare or Concerning Wildlife

Black bears and mountain lions are not expected in this area based on regional surveys. If tracks or scat are discovered, photograph them in place (without handling), note GPS coordinates, and contact the station coordinator immediately.
