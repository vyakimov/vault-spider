# Winter Access Conditions

## North Ridge in Winter

The North Ridge station remains reachable by vehicle throughout December, January, and February, assuming plowing of the main access road continues on the county schedule. Check the county transportation website before departing in a storm; plow schedules may pause for 24+ hours during heavy snow.

The 0.8 km service road from the gate can ice over. Four-wheel drive is recommended if snow is fresh or if temperatures drop below −5°C in the previous 12 hours.

## South Meadow: December Through March Closure

The South Meadow station is not accessible by vehicle from November through March. The primary boardwalk approach becomes unsafe as the wooden structure ices; the East Spur detour route is closed by padlocked gate.

A single foot-access visit is permitted once per month during this period if conditions are evaluated as safe on-site. Winter boots with aggressive tread and handheld electronic beacons (see Field Equipment Checklist) are required.

## Reduced Visit Cadence

Winter visits are scheduled every 2 weeks (rather than weekly in summer) because sensor maintenance takes longer in cold conditions and travel times increase on snow-covered roads. Battery life in sensor electronics also degrades slightly in sustained cold (<−10°C).
