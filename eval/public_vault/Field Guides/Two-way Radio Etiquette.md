# Two-way Radio Etiquette

## Channel Assignments

- **Channel 1 (466.000 MHz)**: Primary field operations channel used for all station visits
- **Channel 2 (466.025 MHz)**: Weather service relay; monitor only, do not transmit
- **Channel 3 (466.050 MHz)**: Backup field channel if primary is congested or compromised

All radios are tuned to Channel 1 before departing for fieldwork. Test transmission at 14:00 local time daily.

## Call Conventions

Identify your transmission with:

- Your name or field-team initials (e.g., "Victor Yankee" or "VY")
- Your current location (e.g., "North Ridge" or "en route to South Meadow")
- Brief status (e.g., "on-site" or "equipment check in progress")

Example: "Victor Yankee, North Ridge, on-site and equipment check in progress. Over."

Wait for acknowledgement before transmitting additional information. Use the word "Over" to indicate the end of your transmission and that you expect a reply. Use "Out" only when terminating the conversation completely.

## Traffic Protocol

Do not interrupt an ongoing transmission, even if there is an emergency. If two transmissions collide (both parties speak at once), wait at least 3 seconds, then try again.

Emergency transmissions override all other traffic. If you hear "EMERGENCY" or "MAYDAY," cease all other radio activity and listen.
