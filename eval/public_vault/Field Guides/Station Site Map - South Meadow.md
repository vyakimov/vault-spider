# Station Site Map - South Meadow

## Boardwalk and Flood Zone

The South Meadow station sits in a seasonal wetland. A raised wooden boardwalk (0.6 metres above summer water level) connects the parking area to the equipment zone. The boardwalk is inspected annually in March and repaired as needed; boards with visible rot or separation must be reported to the maintenance coordinator.

The entire south and east margins of the 60 m × 40 m site become inundated during spring runoff (typically mid-April through May). Equipment is positioned to survive submersion for 2–3 weeks without damage, as devices are sealed IP67 or better.

## Equipment Cabinet Placement

The equipment cabinet (gray steel, locked) is mounted 1.2 metres above the boardwalk surface on a steel post to keep it clear of standing water. This height prevents moisture ingress even during high-water years.

A second weatherproof plastic storage box sits at ground level and contains only consumables: paper towels, cleaning wipes, and spare battery wrappers that can tolerate wet conditions.

## Gateway and Sensor Arrangement

The gateway device is installed in a high-mount enclosure 2 metres north of the equipment cabinet, also elevated. Two sensor clusters radiate outward on their own submersible posts: one in the wet meadow (soil and water properties) and one at the meadow edge (air measurements).

During spring melt, inspect all post footings and cable exits after each visit to confirm nothing has shifted.
