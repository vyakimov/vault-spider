# Weather Radio Frequencies

## NOAA All-Hazards Frequencies

National Weather Radio (NWR) operates on these broadcast-only channels in our region:

- 162.550 MHz: Primary county forecast and warnings
- 162.475 MHz: Backup backup when 162.550 is congested during severe weather
- 162.425 MHz: Marine conditions, reserved for coastal observations only

Reception at North Ridge is strong; South Meadow gets intermittent dropouts during afternoon thermals.

## Marine and Aviation Channels

The coastal spur access route requires monitoring a dedicated marine channel when equipment reaches the secondary trailhead near tide line:

- 161.550 MHz: National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration marine traffic coordination
- 121.500 MHz: Aviation emergency frequency (monitor only; do not transmit without licensed pilot)

The marine frequency is used only for situational awareness during the March–May monsoon season when the creek crossing becomes impassable.

## Equipment Maintenance

Handheld and fixed radios should be tested at the start of each field day at 14:00 local time on the primary frequency. Keep spare 9-volt batteries in the equipment cabinet because salt spray and humidity drain them faster than shelf life predicts.
