# Field Notebook Conventions

## Paper vs. Digital

Field teams use waterproof paper notebooks for on-site documentation and a tablet app (Fieldwire) for redundant record-keeping. The paper notebook is the primary record; the tablet backs it up in case of device failure. Both must agree by end of day.

Spiral-bound waterproof notebooks are kept in the equipment cabinet. Do not bring notebook paper to the field; water damage can render notes illegible.

## Shorthand for Sensor Readings

Use the following abbreviations in field notes:

- **ATH**: Air temperature and humidity (always collected together from the northern cluster)
- **RG**: Rainfall gauge (emptied and reset after reading)
- **SMR**: Soil moisture reading (at three depths: 0-5 cm, 15 cm, 30 cm)
- **BAT**: Battery voltage of the gateway device (should be 12.0V or higher)
- **ERR**: Error flags observed during the visit (e.g., "ERR-01 = missed 5-min reading window")

Example note line: "2026-07-15 14:30 NR ATH 23.4C 67% RG 2.3mm BAT 12.1V"

## Page Conventions

Write one visit per page. Number pages sequentially in the back cover. If a page is damaged or contains errors, cross it out with a single line (legibility for later review) and note "void" in the margin. Do not tear out pages.

At the end of each season (October 31), submit the completed notebook to the station coordinator for archival. Completed notebooks are stored in the office filing cabinet, indexed by year and field team member name.
