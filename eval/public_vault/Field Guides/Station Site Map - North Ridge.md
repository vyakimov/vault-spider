# Station Site Map - North Ridge

## Site Layout

The North Ridge station occupies a 40 m × 30 m cleared area on a flat ridgeline facing northwest. The access road enters from the south and terminates at a small gravel parking area.

### Equipment Locations

**Equipment cabinet** (gray welded steel, locked): Located 15 metres north of the parking area. Houses power banks, spare batteries, USB cables, and backup sensor modules. Key kept at the station office; do not attempt to breach the cabinet in an emergency—call the office first.

**Solar array** (three 100 W panels on adjustable frame): Positioned 8 metres west of the cabinet on a concrete pad. Summer orientation is 180 degrees (due south); winter angle is increased to 45 degrees above horizontal.

**Gateway device Cedar** (mounted in weatherproof enclosure): Bolted to a post 4 metres east of the solar array. Cable runs underground to the equipment cabinet for protection.

### Sensor Clusters

**Northern cluster**: Two instruments on a 2 metre mast, 10 metres from the gateway. Records air temperature and relative humidity at 5-minute intervals.

**Southern cluster**: Soil-moisture and rainfall sensor, 5 metres south of the northern cluster. Rain gauge is at 1.5 metres height and must be cleared of debris after each storm.

## Seasonal Maintenance

The site is mowed by a contractor in May and September to prevent shrub encroachment. Do not disturb the equipment during mowing; vacate the site or remain in the equipment cabinet area.
