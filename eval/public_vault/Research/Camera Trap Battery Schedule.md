# Camera Trap Battery Schedule

## Maintenance Cycle and Intervals

Five camera traps deployed across the study area follow a staggered maintenance schedule. Each camera is visited on a 6-week cycle (every other week for the full array), coinciding with stream velocity surveys to maximize field team efficiency. Battery type (8 AA Energizer MAX alkaline) is standardized across all units to simplify logistics and storage.

## SD Card and Battery Replacement

Depleted batteries are replaced on-site and carried out in labeled bags. SD cards are swapped for pre-formatted blanks during each visit; full cards are downloaded and archived at the research station. Average battery duration is 35–42 days under active triggering (50–100 events per day across the five sites), shorter during warm months when insect movement increases motion detection frequency.

## Power Management and Contingency

Time-lapse mode is disabled during high-activity seasons (May–September) to reduce unnecessary power draw. Night-vision mode draws power proportional to infrared flash intensity; low-intensity mode is used for close cameras (< 5 m expected range) to extend battery life. Backup batteries are stored in a waterproof ammo box at the weather station, with inventory checked quarterly.
