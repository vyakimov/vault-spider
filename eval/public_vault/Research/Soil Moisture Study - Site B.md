# Soil Moisture Study - Site B

## Site B Overview and Motivation

Site B was established in 2023 to extend the soil moisture study across a contrasting soil texture and aspect. Located 800 m southeast of the primary study plot at a south-facing slope, Site B occupies a sandy loam vs. the clay loam of Site A. This comparison allows investigation of how soil water holding capacity affects seasonal dynamics and response to rainfall events.

## Sampling Protocol for Site B

Soil samples are collected Mondays at 14:00 through the growing season (April–October), providing a different temporal sampling pattern than Site A. Samples are extracted at 15 cm and 45 cm depths using a hand auger, capturing both upper-soil drying cycles and mid-depth recharge. Prior-week rainfall is recorded, but Site B receives less precipitation due to its slope aspect and elevation (12 m lower than Site A).

## Comparative Analysis

Initial results show Site B reaches peak volumetric water content of 0.18 cm³/cm³ compared to 0.22 cm³/cm³ at Site A, consistent with the lower clay fraction. Winter saturation is less persistent; water drains through the sandy profile more rapidly. This site documents the extremes of water retention and informs interpretation of the primary plot's intermediate behavior.
