# Stream Gauge Maintenance Log

## Monthly Inspection Schedule

The stream gauge undergoes inspection on the first Tuesday of each month between 08:00 and 10:00. Sediment blockages in the intake are cleared using a soft brush and filtered water rinse. The float mechanism is checked for binding, and the recording pen is inspected for ink flow and paper feed alignment.

## Calibration Protocol

Every quarter, the gauge is calibrated against a precision staff gauge mounted 2 meters downstream. Zero-point verification is performed by lowering the float to the gauge housing floor and confirming the recording pen marks 0.00 m. Linearity is checked at 0.5 m intervals up to 1.5 m, with ±0.02 m tolerance.
