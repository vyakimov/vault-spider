# Research Data Sharing Policy

## Open-Access Datasets and Licensing

All meteorological data (temperature, precipitation, wind speed) are released under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license, enabling unrestricted reuse with citation. Time series are provided at daily and hourly resolution. Biotic datasets (bird surveys, phenology observations) are released annually with a 2-year embargo following collection to allow analysis priority for primary researchers. See [[Long-Term Monitoring Plan]], [[Camera Trap Deployment Notes]], [[Rainfall Gauge Comparison]], and [[Water Quality Sampling Protocol]] for specific datasets.

## Data Repository and Metadata Standards

All datasets are deposited in the Zenodo open repository within 6 months of publication. Metadata files follow the ISO 19115 geographic information standard. Data quality flags are included to identify missing values, calibration periods, and instrumental drift corrections. Processing scripts for data quality assurance are published alongside raw data to enable reproducibility.

## Restrictions and Sensitive Information

Wildlife camera data (images and video) are subsampled for public release, removing identifying features of sensitive infrastructure. See [[Research Photo Archive Conventions]], [[Bird Survey Protocol]], and [[Research Data Sharing Policy]] documentation for privacy-protection procedures. Volunteer names and contact information are never included in archived datasets.
