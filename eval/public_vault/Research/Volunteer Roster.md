# Volunteer Roster

## Current Active Volunteers

- **Bird Survey Protocol trainers** (6 volunteers): Core team with 2–5 years experience; authorized to lead public survey events. Lead bi-weekly walks May–September. Minimum annual commitment: 36 hours.
- **Invasive species removal crew** (4 volunteers): Trained in knapweed hand-pulling and Japanese knotweed herbicide application. Summer intensive program; 8 hours/week June–September.
- **Phenology observers** (3 volunteers): Monitor specific trees; report weekly budburst and senescence events. Year-round engagement; minimal time commitment (< 4 hours/month).
- **Equipment maintenance support** (2 volunteers): Camera trap and weather station tasks requiring mechanical aptitude. Training required; 6 hours/month.

## Training Requirements by Role

- Bird survey: 8-hour training (classroom and field components)
- Invasive species removal: 4-hour safety and identification training
- Phenology observation: 2-hour briefing on target trees and photo documentation
- Equipment maintenance: 6-hour technical training with senior staff

## Retention and Scheduling

Volunteers are re-trained annually to refresh skills and update protocols. Schedule coordination occurs 2 months in advance; participants receive Google Calendar invitations. Data on volunteer hours and participation trends inform grant reports. See [[Field Season Calendar 2024]] for seasonal activity windows.
