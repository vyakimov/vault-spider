# Field Season Calendar 2024

## Spring Activities (March–May)

- **Mar 1**: Weather station transition to daily observations (increased frequency during spring melt)
- **Apr 1**: Bird survey protocol begins; volunteers trained by April 15
- **Apr 15**: Phenology monitoring starts; weekly observations of bud break
- **May 1**: Pollinator transect walks begin; three dates per month scheduled
- **May 15**: Soil moisture sampling resumes (Tuesdays); winter hiatus ends

## Summer Research (June–August)

- **June 1**: Canopy closure monitoring; hemispherical photography every two weeks
- **Jun 15**: Water quality sampling established at bi-weekly frequency
- **July 4**: Mid-season calibration of weather station instruments
- **Aug 1**: Camera trap battery replacement cycle begins (every 6 weeks)
- **Aug 15**: Tree diameter survey of reference plot (annual re-measurement)

## Fall Transition (September–October)

- **Sep 1**: Bird survey shifts to end-of-summer protocol
- **Sep 15**: Invasive species monitoring for seed production assessment
- **Oct 1**: Phenology observations track leaf senescence and fall coloration
- **Oct 15**: Weather station transitions to reduced winter schedule
- **Oct 31**: Field season closes; equipment winterization begins
