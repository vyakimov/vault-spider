# Snow Depth Measurement Notes

## Measurement Protocol and Frequency

Snow depth is recorded at the weather station and at five distributed points across the study area on each observation day when snow cover exceeds 5 cm. Measurements are taken using a certified snow depth stake or, when snow has settled, by averaging three insertion points with a metric ruler. The measurement is taken at the vertical surface, not along a sloped ground plane.

## Spatial Variability Assessment

Open areas near the weather station typically accumulate 10–30% more snow than sheltered riparian zones, reflecting wind redistribution and canopy interception. A temporary snow course was established along a south-facing slope to quantify melt timing differences; this site loses snow 1–2 weeks earlier than the north-facing reference plot, consistent with increased solar radiation input.

## Integration with Hydrologic Monitoring

Peak snow depth (typically late February) is compared with stream discharge timing to assess snowmelt contribution to winter flow. Snow density sampling (using a Scott volumetric sampler) is conducted monthly in January–March to estimate water content.
