# Soil Moisture Sensor Placement Notes

## Depth Stratification Rationale

Soil moisture sensors are installed at two depths to capture both vadose-zone dynamics and rooting-zone hydrology. The 10 cm sensor is positioned above typical grass root depth and responds rapidly to rainfall and evaporation events. The 30 cm sensor accesses the upper mineral soil, reflecting deeper water movement and seasonal storage. A third sensor at 60 cm was considered but abandoned due to cost and access difficulty in clay-rich lower layers.

## Spatial Array Design

Within the primary study plot, five vertical profiles are installed at 5-meter intervals along a north–south transect. This spacing allows detection of small-scale soil heterogeneity while remaining tractable for quarterly maintenance. An additional profile was added 20 m to the east on a gentle slope to assess aspect effects on soil moisture distribution.

## Sensor Installation and Calibration

Each sensor is embedded in a bentonite plug 5 cm above installation depth to ensure hydraulic contact with surrounding soil. Pre-installation dry calibration uses a desiccator with silica gel; wet calibration is performed by saturating soil samples in the lab and logging sensor output. Sensors are checked annually for drift by comparison with gravimetric samples collected at the same depths.
