# Weather Station Site Selection Notes

## Geographic and Exposure Criteria

The current weather station location was selected after evaluating five candidate sites across the research area. The chosen site sits at 340 m elevation with southern exposure free of tree canopy within a 50 m radius. Wind exposure is unobstructed to the north and west, crucial for accurate anemometer and precipitation measurements. Sites closer to riparian corridors were rejected due to salt aerosol interference during dry conditions.

## Historical Comparison with Predecessors

Two prior station locations were abandoned due to shade encroachment and electromagnetic interference from communications equipment installed in 2018. The current site was verified with magnetic field measurements below 0.5 μT at the instrument height, confirming suitability for sensitive humidity and temperature sensors.

## Soil Foundation and Grounding

The station foundation consists of a concrete pad 1.2 m × 1.2 m × 0.3 m, set below frost line at 0.6 m depth. Electrical grounding uses eight 1.5 m copper rods driven at radial intervals, achieving soil resistance of 8 ohms. Lightning strike risk was modeled using local topography; the station sits on an alluvial bench, not a ridge crest.
