# Weather Station Calibration - Rain Gauge

## Weighing Gauge Calibration Protocol

The rain gauge weighing sensor is calibrated twice annually (March and September) using precision weights traceable to NIST standards. A 500 g calibrated weight placed in the collection bucket corresponds to 50.8 mm of rainfall. The electronic scale is zeroed on an empty, dry collection basin, then read after adding successive weights at 100 g, 300 g, 500 g, and 1000 g increments. Linearity error must not exceed ±1.5% of full scale.

## Evaporation Loss Correction

Monthly baseline measurements (zero rainfall days) are recorded to quantify evaporative losses. During the growing season, evaporation rate averages 0.08 mm per day; during winter dormancy, losses drop to 0.02 mm per day. These rates are subtracted from all recorded rainfall to obtain true precipitation accumulation.

## Physical Maintenance

The collection basin is cleaned monthly using distilled water and soft bristles to remove algae and mineral deposits. Drainage ports (three 2.5 mm orifices) are flushed with pressurized air monthly to prevent sediment blockage. The tipping mechanism (if used in backup configuration) is tested by pouring 25 mL of water into the bucket at 5 mL intervals; each tip should occur at precisely 0.254 mm (0.01 inch) increments.
