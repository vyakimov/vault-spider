# Grant Reporting Notes

## Quarterly Progress Summaries

Grant reports due to NSF are compiled quarterly, summarizing research outputs, publications, and data releases. Performance metrics include volunteer hours logged (averaging 120 hours/quarter), datasets archived (typically 2–4 new datasets per quarter), and peer-reviewed manuscripts (one per year target). Budget expenditures are tracked by category (equipment maintenance, personnel, travel) against approved allocations.

## Key Research Outcomes Documented

Progress reports highlight data collection achievements across multiple protocols. See [[Vegetation Survey Data Sheet Template]] for field methods, [[Snow Depth Measurement Notes]] for winter monitoring, [[Weather Station Calibration - Wind Sensor]] for instrument maintenance, [[Research Equipment Loan Agreement]] for external collaboration, [[Field Season Calendar 2024]] for scheduling coordination, and [[Bird Survey Protocol]] for volunteer engagement metrics.

## Reporting Timeline and Deliverables

Annual comprehensive reports are due May 31 and include: publication list, conference presentations, student training outcomes, and outreach activities. Data management plans are updated annually to reflect new datasets and archive locations. Budget revisions requesting carryover funds must be submitted by April 15.
