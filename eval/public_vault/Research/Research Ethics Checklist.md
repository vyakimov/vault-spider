# Research Ethics Checklist

## Pre-Field Session Review

- [ ] Review current site access agreement with landowner for seasonal restrictions
- [ ] Check weather forecast for hazardous conditions (lightning, heat, hypothermia risk)
- [ ] Verify emergency communication plan (cell service, satellite messenger if needed)
- [ ] Inspect safety equipment: first aid kit, emergency shelter, headlamp batteries
- [ ] Confirm companion/backup contact person and check-in time

## Wildlife and Habitat Considerations

- [ ] Minimize disturbance to nesting birds during April–July (200 m buffer)
- [ ] Avoid trampling riparian vegetation during spring flood stages
- [ ] Report wildlife injuries or unusual behavior to state wildlife agency
- [ ] Remove all trash and equipment from field sites (leave no trace)
- [ ] Inspect camera trap placement for safe tree stability and access routes

## Data Privacy and Security

- [ ] No visitor or volunteer personal information recorded
- [ ] Camera trap images reviewed for identifying features before archival
- [ ] Field notebooks stored securely; transcribed data encrypted on computers
- [ ] Informed consent obtained for any humans incidentally photographed
- [ ] See [[Camera Trap Deployment Notes]] for detailed wildlife camera protocols
