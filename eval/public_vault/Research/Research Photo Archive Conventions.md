# Research Photo Archive Conventions

## Filename and Metadata Structure

All research photographs use a consistent naming convention: `YYYY-MM-DD_SITE_SUBJECT_SEQ.jpg`. Example: `2024-06-15_Plot-A_Phenology_001.jpg` for the first photo documenting phenology at Plot A on June 15, 2024. Metadata embedded in image EXIF records includes: GPS coordinates, camera model, aperture, exposure time, and ISO. A spreadsheet index cross-references filenames with field notebook entries for sample location and study protocol.

## Privacy and Sensitive Information

Photographs containing identifiable people, license plates, or residential structures are excluded from archive or encrypted before storage. Wildlife camera images are reviewed for privacy before inclusion in public datasets; any images containing humans are deleted. Aerial drone photographs (when used) are reviewed for nearby residential addresses and pixelated before release.

## Storage and Backup Systems

Original full-resolution images are stored on a network-attached storage (NAS) device with redundant disk configuration (RAID-1 mirroring). Weekly incremental backups are created to external hard drives stored off-site. Derivative lower-resolution images (for presentations and reports) are stored separately in compressed JPEG format at 1200 pixel maximum dimension. RAW files are retained for 5 years; converted images are archived indefinitely.
