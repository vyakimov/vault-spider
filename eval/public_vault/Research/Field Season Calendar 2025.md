# Field Season Calendar 2025

## Spring Activities (March–May)

- **Mar 1**: Snow depth observations continue; final melt timing documented
- **Mar 15**: Winter weather station maintenance completed; sensors cleared of ice
- **Apr 1**: Bird survey training for new volunteers (2-week intensive program)
- **Apr 20**: Phenology observations transition to daily during early bud break
- **May 1**: Soil moisture Site B sampling resumes (Mondays at 14:00)

## Summer Research (June–August)

- **Jun 1**: Five-day intensive stream ecology sampling (multiple lab analyses)
- **Jun 15**: Pollinator transect expanded from three to four routes (new riparian zone)
- **Jul 1**: Camera trap re-deployment following spring maintenance
- **Jul 15**: Second season of tree diameter re-measurements (growth quantification)
- **Aug 10**: Vegetation survey protocol validation; accuracy check against prior year

## Fall and Winter Transition (September–December)

- **Sep 1**: Fall bird migration monitoring; species composition shifts
- **Sep 20**: Water quality sampling shifts to monthly frequency
- **Oct 5**: Invasive species final treatment season; equipment checkout
- **Oct 30**: Phenology observations of peak senescence; final photo documentation
- **Nov 15**: Weather station instruments removed for winter maintenance and storage
- **Dec 1**: Equipment inventory and maintenance planning for 2026
