# Site Access Agreement - Landowner

## Property Boundaries and Access Routes

Research activities are permitted on a 12-hectare parcel with documented boundary survey (2019). Primary access via ranch road from the north; emergency access from the southern gate (locked, access by key). The weather station location is 200 m west of the northern road; stream corridor access extends 400 m downstream. Hiking on-foot through adjacent forest is permitted April–October; winter access restricted due to avalanche hazard on steep north-facing slopes.

## Liability and Insurance Requirements

The research organization maintains $2 million general liability insurance with property damage coverage. Annual proof of insurance is provided to the landowner by December 31. Volunteers are covered under this policy during authorized research activities. The landowner is named as additional insured on equipment on-site (weather station, camera traps, soil monitoring installations).

## Term and Renewal Conditions

The agreement is valid through December 31, 2026, with automatic annual renewal unless either party provides 60 days written notice. Modifications to research activities (new sites, expanded volunteer programs) require written approval. The landowner has right to request removal of equipment or termination of specific projects with 30 days notice. See [[Weather Station Site Selection Notes]] and [[Bird Survey Protocol]] for designated activity areas.
