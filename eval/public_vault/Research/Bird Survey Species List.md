# Bird Survey Species List

## Common Breeding Species

- **American Robin** (AMRO): Most frequently recorded breeder; territorial calls common in riparian zone during April–July.
- **Black-capped Chickadee** (BCCH): Year-round resident; cavity nester in dead aspens.
- **Song Sparrow** (SOSP): Abundant in shrubland; multiple broods recorded per season.
- **Common Yellowthroat** (COYE): Confined to wetland margins; witchity call diagnostic.
- **Downy Woodpecker** (DOWO): Secondary cavities used; drumming heard February–March.
- **Swainson's Thrush** (SWTH): Spring migrant; occurs mid-May to early June.

## Migratory Transients

- **White-crowned Sparrow** (WCSP): Peak passage April and September; loose flocks.
- **Hermit Thrush** (HETH): Spring and fall migrant; ground forager.
- **Magnolia Warbler** (MAGW): Brief stop-over in May during northbound migration.

## Waterfowl and Waders

- **Green-winged Teal** (GWTE): Early spring arrival in April; documented via [[Rainfall Gauge Comparison]] stream transect notes.
- **Spotted Sandpiper** (SPSA): Breeds along gravel bars; chick broods recorded.
- **Great Blue Heron** (GBHE): Occasional visitor to stream pools; not known to nest on site.
