# Phenology Observation Log

## Spring Budbreak Phenology

Six ponderosa pine and eight Douglas-fir trees near the weather station are tracked annually for spring budbreak timing. In 2024, the first terminal buds broke April 18 for ponderosa (cold spring year) and April 12 for Douglas-fir. Bud breaking is recorded as the day when 50% of sampled branches show visible bud elongation. Air temperature accumulation models (base 5°C) suggest a threshold of 200 degree-days above baseline for ponderosa budbreak.

## Summer Leaf Phenology

Peak canopy closure occurs by June 1 in normal years, confirmed by monthly hemispherical photography at the weather station. New foliage growth continues through mid-June, with full photosynthetic capability achieved by late June. Leaf drop has advanced 3–5 days earlier over the past decade (2014–2024 comparison), consistent with earlier spring warming but delayed fall cooling.

## Autumn Senescence Timing

Leaf fall begins in early September for deciduous species (willows, cottonwoods), with peak color occurring mid-September. Complete leaf drop is typically achieved by mid-October. See [[Weather Station Calibration - Rain Gauge]] for seasonal precipitation patterns that interact with senescence timing.
