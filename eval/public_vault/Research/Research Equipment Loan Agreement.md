# Research Equipment Loan Agreement

## Eligible Equipment and Loan Periods

Portable research equipment (GPS units, camera traps, soil augers, water quality probes) may be loaned to visiting researchers for periods of 2–4 weeks. Equipment requiring electrical power (data loggers, weighing scales, metal detectors) are loaned for shorter periods (3–7 days) to enable timely re-deployment at the primary site. Equipment on permanent loan (anemometers, temperature sensors) are not available for external loan.

## Loan Application and Approval Process

Requests must be submitted 30 days in advance with a detailed research plan describing intended use, expected field conditions, and return timeline. External users must provide liability insurance certification and demonstrate field competency in equipment operation. Staff may require a 2-hour hands-on training session before loan approval.

## Equipment Return and Damage Assessment

Equipment is returned clean and in operational condition. Minor wear (scratches, loose straps) is expected. Damage requiring repair (cracked screens, electronic failures, mechanical wear exceeding normal use) incurs a replacement-cost charge; users are notified of charges before billing.
