# Tree Diameter Survey Protocol

## Measurement Height and Standardization

Diameter at breast height (DBH) is the standard measurement for all trees exceeding 10 cm DBH. Breast height is defined as 1.3 m above the highest ground level at the tree base. For trees on slopes, ground level is taken as the upslope point. Measurements are taken perpendicular to the main axis of lean, using a diameter tape to the nearest 0.1 cm.

## Instrument Specifications

A metric diameter tape (Forestry Suppliers model DBH30) provides direct DBH readout without conversion from circumference. The tape is calibrated annually against a machined steel cylinder of known diameter (±0.05 cm tolerance). For non-round trees (multiple stems, buttressing), the geometric mean of two perpendicular diameters is recorded.

## Repeat Measurement Protocols

Trees are marked with numbered aluminum tags (0.5 m below DBH height) to enable precise repeat measurements across years. A reference plot of 30 trees is re-measured annually to quantify growth rates and detect measurement error. Average annual growth for ponderosa pine in the study area is 0.3–0.5 cm/year.
