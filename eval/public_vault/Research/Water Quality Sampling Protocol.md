# Water Quality Sampling Protocol

## Sample Collection Procedures

Water samples are collected from the main stem stream at a fixed cross-section 50 m downstream of the weather station. Three samples are taken per visit: left bank, mid-channel, and right bank, each at 0.4 depth (40% down from the surface). Samples are collected in pre-rinsed 500 mL polypropylene bottles and immediately placed in a cooler with ice packs at 4°C or lower.

## Holding Times and Analysis Windows

Temperature is measured at the time of collection using a calibrated thermometer; all samples must be analyzed within 24 hours of collection for dissolved oxygen and pH. Nutrient samples (nitrogen, phosphorus) are preserved with sulfuric acid and can be held for 7 days. Bacterial samples are analyzed within 6 hours of collection, requiring express transport to the county lab when the overnight analysis window is not available.

## Analytical Methods and Equipment Calibration

Dissolved oxygen is measured using the Winkler titration method (reference standard). Conductivity probes are calibrated daily using 1413 μS/cm standard solutions. The pH meter is two-point calibrated using pH 7.0 and 10.0 buffer solutions before each field day.
